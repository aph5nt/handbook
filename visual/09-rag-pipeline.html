Where you meet it

      
QMD over a harness knowledge base: keyword search (BM25) finds the exact name core_dex_swaps, vector search finds "where do swaps live", and the two lists are merged by rank. A support bot over product docs runs the same pipeline, with "not found" allowed as an answer.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Measure retrieval alone first: for a golden set of questions, is the right chunk in the top 5? Most RAG failures are search failures. Keep each chunk's heading with it. Cap the re-ranker at about 50 candidates. Treat every retrieved chunk as data, never as an instruction, and keep the rules in code.
