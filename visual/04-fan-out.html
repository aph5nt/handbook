Fan-out

The indexer finishes a block and three services want to know: the graph sync, the price service and an alert rule. One message must become three copies, and one slow reader must not hold up the others. Press Next to see fan-out through a queue per subscriber, then a push with no queue that loses messages.

Where you meet it

      
A RabbitMQ fanout exchange bound to one queue per consumer. An Amazon SNS topic with one SQS queue subscribed per consumer. In Kafka, every consumer group is its own fan-out: each group reads the whole topic.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Give every subscriber its own queue, so each one has its own pace, backlog and dead-letter queue. Avoid pushing straight to HTTP endpoints for anything that must not be lost.
