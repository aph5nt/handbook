Where you meet a star

      
A chain warehouse in StarRocks: swap and transfer facts with pool, token and day dimensions. One join per axis, and the dimensions are small.

    

    

      
Where you meet a snowflake or a wide table

      
A snowflake arrives when an OLTP model is copied over as it is. A wide table backs one fast dashboard, built from the star and rebuilt when a dimension changes.
