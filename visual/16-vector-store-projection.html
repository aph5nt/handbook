Vector stores as a projection

Where you meet it: retrieval for an LLM

      
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation): document chunks are embedded and searched by similarity. When the documents or the model change, the index is rebuilt.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: address similarity

      
Transfer facts are turned into address vectors for fraud work. pgvector in Postgres fits small sets, a dedicated vector store fits large ones. Either way it is rebuilt from facts.
