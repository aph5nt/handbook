Medallion layers: bronze, silver, gold

Raw blocks land in bronze. The indexer decodes them into typed facts in silver. A daily job sums them into a small gold table that the dashboards read. Press Next to see a decoder fix replayed through the layers, and what goes wrong when readers skip gold.

Where you meet it: a chain pipeline

      
Captured blocks and receipts are bronze. Decoded swaps and transfers in StarRocks are silver. Daily volume per exchange is gold. A decoder change rebuilds silver from bronze, then gold.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: any lakehouse

      
Databricks and Iceberg setups use the same three names. Bronze for raw ingestion, silver for cleaned tables, gold for business marts that reports read.
