Where you meet it: retry libraries

      
HTTP clients, gateways and service meshes retry GET, PUT and DELETE on a timeout, and never a POST without a key.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: settings and Kubernetes

      
A settings page sends PATCH with only the changed fields, so two editors of different fields do not collide. kubectl apply sends a patch, and the object's version stops stale writes.

    

  


  

    
Side by side

    

      
MethodSafeIdempotentRetry after a timeout

      
GET, HEADYesYesAlways fine

      
PUTNoYesFine, if the body is the whole object

      
DELETENoYesFine, may answer 404

      
PATCHNoOnly if it sets valuesFine for set, harmful for add

      
POSTNoNoOnly with an idempotency key

      
Interview line"Safe changes nothing, idempotent can be repeated, PUT replaces the whole thing, and a PATCH is only safe to retry when it sets values instead of adding to them."
