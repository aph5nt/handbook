Where you meet it

      
After a bulk load or a new day partition in a warehouse: collect statistics before the first report. StarRocks chooses broadcast or shuffle for swaps JOIN pools from the estimated size of pools. An engine upgrade can change plans, so diff the plans of hot queries.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Run ANALYZE after every load. When a plan is slow, compare estimated with actual rows first. Where two columns move together, such as city and postcode, add multi-column statistics instead of a hint.
