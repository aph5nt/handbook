Where you meet domains

      
Any company past a few dozen services. Each domain gets one gateway, other domains call only that, and layers point one way. At home: the indexer, the warehouse and the graph each expose one surface, the proxy for agents and the SQL views for analysts. Nobody reaches into an inner table.

    

    

      
Where you meet cells

      
Amazon cells, Shopify pods, Uber's later zones. A cell is a full copy of the stack for a slice of users. Deploy to one cell first. A cell is a known size, so you can test it, add one to scale, and lose one without losing everyone.
