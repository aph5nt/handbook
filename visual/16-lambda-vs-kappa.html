Where you meet lambda

      
Older big-data stacks: Hadoop or Spark batch for history beside Storm or Flink for live numbers. You accept it when the source cannot be replayed and history must be recomputed from a separate store.

    

    

      
Where you meet kappa

      
Kafka with long retention, or a blockchain, as the replayable log. A chain indexer follows the head and rebuilds history by replaying from block 1 through the same code. Nightly batch jobs read its tables.
