Data observability

The swap facts table fills hour by hour. Things go wrong in ways a job status never shows: a stall, a missing exchange, a changed column, empty prices. Press Next and watch which monitor catches each one.

Where you meet it: a chain warehouse

      
Freshness is newest block time against the chain head. Volume is rows per hour against past days. Reconciliation compares row counts per block range with the chain. Alerts go to the table owner.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: tools

      
dbt tests and Great Expectations check values in the pipeline. Monte Carlo and Soda watch freshness and volume from outside. Prometheus and Grafana work too, with a query per monitor.
