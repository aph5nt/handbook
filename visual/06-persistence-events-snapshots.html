Where you meet it

      
Akka Persistence and Akka.NET Persistence, with a journal in PostgreSQL, Cassandra or SQL Server. Orleans grain storage and event-sourced grains. Event sourcing in any service: the same idea, without actors. In the IoT backend, configuration and connection events went to the journal and readings went to the warehouse.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
What is an event? Persist decisions: a change of configuration, a connection, an alarm raised. Do not persist raw data that only flows through. Pick a snapshot interval so recovery replays a few hundred events, not millions.
