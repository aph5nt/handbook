HyperLogLog

How many distinct wallets traded today, out of billions of transfers? An exact set costs gigabytes. HyperLogLog keeps only the longest run of leading zeros seen in the address hashes, spread over registers. Hash bits here are short and made up, to show the idea.

Where you meet HyperLogLog

      
Redis PFADD and PFCOUNT: distinct counts in 12 KB per key, about 0.81% error. approx_count_distinct in StarRocks, Presto and BigQuery, far lighter than COUNT(DISTINCT ...) on billions of rows.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Store one HLL per day and merge any range at query time. Use a Bloom filter for "is this one there?" and an exact set when you need the names. Avoid intersections by subtraction: the errors add up.
