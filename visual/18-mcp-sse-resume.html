Where you meet it

      
Long tool calls with progress. A GET stream for server-sent notifications such as list_changed to a remote client. A short call needs no stream: plain JSON.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Give every event an id. Keep a short replay buffer per stream. To stop work, the client sends notifications/cancelled; closing the stream is not enough.
