Active-active and active-passive: who serves when a region dies

Two regions hold the same data. In active-active both serve users. In active-passive one serves and one waits behind a pointer. Watch a region die in each, and watch the failure each one must guard against: a write conflict, and split brain. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Two identical serving lanes with a pointer that names the live one: active-passive with a rehearsed flip. DynamoDB global tables and Cassandra across regions: active-active with last write wins. A pilot light for a back-office tool where an hour of downtime is fine and an idle second region is not.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Let the RTO (how long you may be down) and the budget choose. Active-active needs capacity for a whole lost region and a plan for conflicts: a home region per record, or a merge rule. Active-passive needs one authority for who is live, with fencing, and a failover practised often. Warm standby and pilot light trade failover time for cost.
