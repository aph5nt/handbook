Tail latency and hedged requests

A wallet page asks 100 storage shards for its rows and must wait for all of them. Each shard is slow only once in a hundred calls. Press Next to see why the page is still slow most of the time, and how a hedged request fixes it.

Where you meet it

      
Google's paper "The Tail at Scale" introduced hedged and tied requests for Bigtable-style systems. Cassandra's speculative retry sends a read that is slower than the p99 to another replica. Any search or feed page that fans out to many shards lives on its tail.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Measure p99, not the average. Hedge only idempotent reads, only after about the p95 time, and cap the extra load at a few percent. Never hedge a write such as a payment: it would happen twice.
