Where you meet it

      
An indexer calling a node over RPC (remote procedure call). A proxy with a 10-second budget that passes the time left to the graph database, so a slow query dies there instead of finishing an answer nobody will read. Payments, where a client idempotency key turns a retried charge into one charge.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Give every call a timeout shorter than your own deadline. Pass the deadline down to each hop. Retry in one layer only, with a cap of a few tries and a retry budget of about 10 percent extra calls. Retry only idempotent calls. Put a circuit breaker on top.
