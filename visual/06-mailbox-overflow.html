Mailbox overflow and back-pressure

A device actor receives 1,000 readings a second and can persist 200 a second. With a plain tell nothing slows the sender, so the mailbox grows in memory. Press Next and watch an hour pass. Then try a bounded mailbox, and a stream where the writer's demand slows the source.

Where you meet it

      
Any actor fed by a plain tell from a faster producer: telemetry, log lines, webhooks. Akka bounded mailboxes, Akka Streams and Reactive Streams, Go bounded channels. Mailbox depth is the health signal of an actor system: watch it and alert on it.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Telemetry ingest: a stream with demand end to end, so a slow writer slows the socket. An actor that must stay an actor: a bounded mailbox with a drop rule and a dead-letter metric. Never a plain tell from a fast source with nothing in between.
