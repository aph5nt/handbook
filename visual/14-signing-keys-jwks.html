Where you meet key pairs

      
Any token read by more than one service, and every OIDC provider: RS256 or ES256, with keys at a JWKS URL found through the provider's discovery document.

    

    

      
Where a shared secret is fine

      
One service that signs and checks its own cookies or tokens, with nobody else verifying. HS256 is simple and fast there.
