Where you meet it: a chain warehouse

      
Swap and transfer facts in StarRocks primary-key tables, keyed by block height and log index. A re-index or a replay overwrites, never duplicates.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: change data capture and queues

      
Debezium rows from Postgres are upserted by primary key with the log position as the version. A Kafka consumer deduplicates by event id before it sends an email.
