Where you meet each one

      
At-most-once for metrics samples and presence pings: the next message replaces the lost one. At-least-once for anything with money, always with an idempotent consumer. Kafka's auto-commit of offsets on a timer can quietly turn at-least-once into at-most-once.

    

    

      
What "exactly-once" really is

      
Kafka's idempotent producer and transactions give exactly-once inside Kafka. The moment the effect leaves Kafka, into a database or a card network, only an idempotent consumer helps. An EVM indexer that upserts rows by (block, tx_index, log_index) is effectively-once.
