Immutable images by digest

A team tests indexer v1.2.3 on staging, then rebuilds it for production. The base image moved in between. Press Next to watch a tag move, then open the second tab to see a digest promoted unchanged.

Where you meet it: lane values files

      
Every lane values file names images by digest. A release is built on demand, once, and the same digest moves from the staging lane to production.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: supply chain

      
A signing step signs the digest at build, and the cluster checks the signature at deploy. A tag cannot be signed in a way that stays true.
