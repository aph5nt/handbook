Where you meet it

      
Resilience4j in Java (Netflix Hystrix before it), gobreaker in Go. An indexer calling a chain node's RPC (Remote Procedure Call) endpoint with a timeout and backoff. Separate pools for warehouse and graph in one API, so a slow graph query fails alone.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Give every call a deadline. Retry only idempotent calls, at one layer only, with exponential backoff, jitter and a retry budget such as at most 10 % extra calls. Put a breaker in front of each dependency, and give each one its own pool.

    

  


  

    
Side by side

Interview line"Every call fails sooner or later. I give it a deadline, retry it with jitter at one layer, and stop calling a sick service with a breaker."
