Where you meet B-trees

      
Every relational index is a B+ tree: one structure for the point lookup and the range scan. Monotonic keys like block_height append at the right edge. Random keys like UUIDs split pages everywhere.

    

    

      
Where you meet heaps

      
Top k of a billion rows: a min-heap of size k, one pass, O(n log k). ORDER BY amount DESC LIMIT 10 is the same heap inside the engine. A job scheduler and Dijkstra's frontier use one too.
