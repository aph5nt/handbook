Chaos engineering: a controlled experiment in production

Netflix believes that losing one Playback copy should not change how many films start each second. Instead of hoping, it tests the belief on a small share of real traffic. Watch one experiment pass and the next one find a weakness. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Chaos Mesh and Litmus inject failures into Kubernetes. AWS Fault Injection Service does it for AWS resources. Amazon and many others run GameDays: planned sessions where a team breaks one thing and practises the response.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Start on staging, then a tiny share of production, with an automatic stop on the steady-state number. Rehearse your biggest switch every release, such as flipping traffic between two serving lanes.
