Service-based architecture

The indexer, the price oracle and the validator deploy on their own, but share one StarRocks cluster. Each owns its own database in it. A handful of coarse services and one database server is service-based architecture. Press Next.

Where you meet it: one warehouse, many writers

      
Several services on one StarRocks or Postgres server, each with its own database or schema. Readers from another service get a view, not the tables.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: a first split from a monolith

      
Mark Richards describes service-based as the usual first step out of a monolith: carve out a few coarse domain services, keep one database server, and split data later only where needed.
