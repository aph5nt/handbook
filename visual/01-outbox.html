Where you meet it

      
A Go indexer that writes a block to StarRocks and an outbox row in one load, then a relay feeds the graph sync. Debezium reading the Postgres write-ahead log and publishing outbox rows to Kafka. Any service that must tell other teams about a change it just saved.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Write the event row in the same transaction as the change. Publish with a relay that retries. Accept at-least-once delivery and make every consumer idempotent. Delete sent rows on a schedule so the table stays small.
