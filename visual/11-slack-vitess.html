Where you meet it

      
Vitess began at YouTube and now shards MySQL at Slack, GitHub, Square and PlanetScale. Slack's edge gateways and its Flannel cache keep WebSocket connections and boot data close to users, while the core stays in the US.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Pick a shard key that spreads load: Slack moved messages from "by workspace" to "by channel". Let a proxy route queries so the app does not know where shards live. Cache what every client asks for at start-up near the user.
