Where you meet it

      
A hash join whose build side is the big table: swap the sides and the spill goes away. GROUP BY over millions of distinct keys: aggregate per partition first. StarRocks with spill turned on: a big query finishes slowly instead of failing with out of memory.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Read the plan for "external merge" or spill bytes. Cut rows first: filter, top-N with LIMIT, pre-aggregate. Raise the memory budget only for the session that needs it.
