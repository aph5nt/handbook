Raft: how five nodes pick one leader

Five nodes keep one copy of the same log, like the three or five members of etcd under a Kubernetes control plane. Only the leader takes writes. Watch what happens when the leader dies, and why the maths of a majority keeps the cluster safe.

Where you meet it

      
etcd under Kubernetes, Consul, CockroachDB, TiKV, and the causal cluster of a Neo4j-family graph database. Three nodes survive one loss. Five survive two. Four survive only one, same as three, so never run an even count.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Spread the nodes across failure zones so a partition cuts off a minority, never half. Keep the election timeout well above the network round trip, or followers start elections while the leader is alive. etcd's default is 1000 ms with a 100 ms heartbeat.
