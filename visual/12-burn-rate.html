Where you meet it

      
A freshness SLO (service level objective) for chain data, with burn measured on minutes over 100 blocks behind head. Prometheus recording rules for error ratios over 5 minutes, 1 hour, 6 hours and 3 days, with alert rules on top. The multi-window, multi-burn-rate recipe from the Google SRE (site reliability engineering) workbook.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Page on a fast burn (about 14 times over an hour, with a 5-minute window to confirm it is still happening). Ticket on a slow burn (about 1 to 3 times over days, with a 6-hour window). Tie every page to an SLO. Delete fixed thresholds that page without costing budget.
