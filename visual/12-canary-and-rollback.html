Where you meet blue-green

      
Two identical serving lanes with a pointer that names the live one. A release goes to the standby lane, is proven there, then the pointer flips. Rollback is flipping it back. It costs double capacity, and both sides must read the same data.

    

    

      
Where you meet a canary

      
Kubernetes with a weighted route, Argo Rollouts, or Zuul at Netflix. Ship one immutable image pinned by digest, send a small share, compare its error rate and p99 to the old version, then ramp. A bad deploy burns the error budget fastest, which is why canaries exist.
