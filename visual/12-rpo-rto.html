Where you meet it

      
A chain-data warehouse can be re-indexed from the chain itself: a loose RPO is fine, because the chain is the backup, but the re-index time is its RTO. A payments ledger with a sync replica in a second zone. Kubernetes volume snapshots on a timer, with a restore drill whose time is written down.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Ask the business for both numbers per data set, not one for the whole company. Pick the copy (backup, async replica, sync replica) for the RPO and the recovery (restore, promote, already serving) for the RTO. Restore and fail over on a schedule, and write the measured times next to the targets.
