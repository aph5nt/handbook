Queue-based load leveling: the queue takes the burst

At market open, agents send three times the usual number of graph jobs for a few minutes. The workers can do 100 a minute. A queue between them takes the burst and the workers drain it at their own pace. Then watch arrivals stay above the drain rate, and the queue grow forever. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Kafka or a cloud queue between an API that accepts jobs and workers that run graph walks. A Go indexer's fetched-block queue between the node and the writer. Email and report generation: accept at once, do it within minutes.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Use it for work that may finish later than the request: reports, syncs, enrichment. Answer the caller with a job id and a status endpoint. Size workers for the average plus margin, not the peak. Watch consumer lag, the age of the oldest job, and scale workers on it.
