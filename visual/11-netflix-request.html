Where you meet it

      
Any service that calls three others to build one answer. Wrap each call with a timeout, a breaker and a fallback. Netflix used Hystrix for this, then moved to resilience4j and adaptive concurrency limits in 2018, because fixed thresholds aged badly.

    

    

      
The same split at home

      
Keep the heavy bytes away from the control path. A blockchain indexer can run its warehouse and graph in two identical lanes, with a pointer naming the live one. Switching lanes is rehearsed every release, like a Netflix region move.
