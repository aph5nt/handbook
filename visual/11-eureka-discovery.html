Where you meet it

      
Kubernetes Services and cluster DNS, Consul, and AWS Cloud Map all do service discovery today. Most service meshes keep a cached list in a sidecar, the same idea as Eureka's client cache.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
For a registry, choose availability: stale entries plus client retries beat a registry that refuses to answer. Cache the list in the client, so the registry can be down for a minute and calls still flow.
