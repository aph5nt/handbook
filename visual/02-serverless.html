Where you meet it: webhooks and file events

      
A payment webhook, a thumbnail on upload, a nightly report: AWS Lambda, Google Cloud Functions or Azure Functions. Short work, triggered by an event, idle most of the day.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: what stays a service

      
An indexer, a graph sync and a database stay long-running services. They hold state, connections and a position, and they run all day.
