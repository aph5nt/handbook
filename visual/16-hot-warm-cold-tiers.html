Hot, warm and cold tiers

The transfers table in StarRocks is partitioned by day. New days sit on fast SSD. A nightly tiering job moves older days to cheaper disks, then to object storage. Press Next and watch partitions cool, and what happens to a table that has no rule.

Where you meet it: a warehouse

      
StarRocks sets a storage medium per partition. A tiering job moves day partitions from SSD to hard disk by age, and old years go to object storage behind an external catalog.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: elsewhere

      
S3 storage classes move objects from standard to infrequent access to archive. Kafka tiered storage keeps recent segments on brokers and old ones in object storage.
