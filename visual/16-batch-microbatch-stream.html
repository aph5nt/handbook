Where you meet it: a chain indexer

      
The chain follow is micro-batch: small block ranges in a loop, each rerunnable. Partition moves and quality reports are nightly batch. A full history rebuild reuses the follower's code from block 1.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: elsewhere

      
Spark Structured Streaming is micro-batch. Flink and Kafka Streams handle each event. Airflow schedules batch. A fraud check needs seconds, a finance report needs the morning.
