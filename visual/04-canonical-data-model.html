Where you meet it

      
Integration platforms and enterprise service buses, a shared Price or Order event format on a company-wide topic, FIX in trading, and HL7 FHIR in health care.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Keep the canonical model small and owned by one team, one per bounded context. Each consumer may still translate it into its own model with an anti-corruption layer (see chapter 1).
