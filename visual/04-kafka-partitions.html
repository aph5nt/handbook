Where you meet partitions

      
Kafka, Redpanda and Pulsar all order messages per partition. Key by the thing that needs order: an account, a wallet, an order id. A graph sync keyed by address keeps the credits and debits of one address in order, with parallelism across addresses.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Pick the partition count early. It caps how many consumers can work at once. Adding partitions later re-hashes keys and breaks order for a while. Watch lag, not just errors: lag is the queue telling you it is falling behind.
