Where you meet hashing

      
Every login: argon2id or bcrypt, salted, slow. Migrate old hashes by wrapping them at once and upgrading at each login.

    

    

      
Where you meet a vault

      
Helm charts reference a Kubernetes Secret object, never the value. Images are built from pinned public commits with no token. Logging middleware redacts known header and field names.
