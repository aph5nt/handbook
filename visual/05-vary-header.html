Where you meet it

      
Vary: Accept-Encoding on every compressed text response. Vary: Accept or Accept-Language for content negotiation. CDNs such as Cloudflare and Fastly let you normalise headers at the edge before they reach the key.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Vary on the few headers that really change the answer, normalised to few values. Never vary on User-Agent or Cookie at a shared edge. Per-user answers should be private, not varied on Authorization.
