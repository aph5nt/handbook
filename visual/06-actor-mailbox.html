One actor, one mailbox, one message at a time

An IoT (Internet of Things) backend on Akka.NET has one actor per device. Device 42 sends two readings at the same moment, on two threads, and a config change arrives on a third. All three land in device 42's mailbox. Press Next and watch why no lock is needed. The second tab shows shared state smuggled back in.

Where you meet it

      
Akka and Akka.NET actors, Erlang processes, Orleans grains. One actor per device, session, cart, connection or workflow instance. Not one big actor for all devices: that is a single-threaded bottleneck with extra steps.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Messages are records that nobody can change after sending. State lives in one actor only. A total across many devices belongs to its own actor, which the devices tell, never to a row they all update.
