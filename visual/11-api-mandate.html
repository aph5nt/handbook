Where you meet it

      
Every service-oriented company follows some form of the mandate: each service owns its data, and others call its API. AWS itself is the mandate turned outward: internal interfaces designed so outside customers could use them.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Each repository owns its tables. Others read through a view or an API, never by joining a stranger's table. Keep teams small, and make the team that builds a service the one that is on call for it.
