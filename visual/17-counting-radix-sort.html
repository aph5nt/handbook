Counting sort and radix sort

Transfers must be sorted by a small number: a day partition, or a short id. These sorts never compare two rows. They read each key's value and drop the row straight into place.

Where you meet them

      
Bucketing rows by a small partition key, such as a day number or a shard id. Sorting millions of fixed-width integers or IP addresses with radix sort. Suffix arrays and GPU sort libraries.

    

    

      
Why they beat n log n

      
The O(n log n) floor holds only for sorts that compare two items at a time. Counting and radix sort read the key's value directly, so they cost O(n + k) and O(d × (n + base)).
