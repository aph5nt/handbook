Where you meet it

      
Consistent hashing places data in Cassandra and Dynamo, picks a server in memcached clients, and balances load in Envoy's ring hash. Gossip membership runs in Cassandra, Consul and Serf.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Prefer a store that owns its partitioning over a ring inside your app. Build your own ring only when in-memory state per key is the product, and plan for split views.
