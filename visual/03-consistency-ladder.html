Where you meet it

      
Every database with read replicas: Postgres, MySQL, a StarRocks follower. Reads go to the replica because it is cheap. A wallet balance after a deposit needs read-your-writes. A recent swaps list on a block explorer is fine on eventual.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Pick a rung per endpoint. Route a client's next read to the leader, or wait until the replica reaches the write's log position. Put a uniqueness check (one username, one lease holder) on the top rung, through a consensus store such as etcd.
