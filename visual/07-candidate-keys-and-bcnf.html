Where you meet it

      
Schema review of an app database: list every column that decides another, then ask whether each is a key. Data modelling interviews ask for the gap between 3NF and BCNF. Warehouses skip it on purpose, because they rebuild every copy from the source.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Split to BCNF when the repeated fact changes or can be lost. Stay in 3NF when the split would lose a rule you need, such as "one router per trader per exchange", and enforce the repeat with a check instead.
