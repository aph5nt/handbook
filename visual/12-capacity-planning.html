Where you meet it

      
Before a launch, a sale or a token event. When choosing how many StarRocks backends or graph replicas a lane needs. Each quarter, from the growth trend, because a new server takes weeks to buy, rack and burn in.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Forecast the peak, not the average. Find each server's safe load with a load test on the real query mix, stopping at the knee where p99 climbs, not at the crash. Keep the peak under that knee. Add N+1 so one failed server at peak is survivable. Order with the lead time in mind.
