Where you meet it

      
Akka remote sends are at most once. Akka's reliable delivery and at-least-once delivery add the journal and the resend. Kafka consumers, SQS (Simple Queue Service) and webhooks are at least once too: every one of them can hand you the same message twice.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Per message type, not per system. Readings: at most once, because a newer one replaces a lost one. Commands that change state: at least once, with a command id. Make the receiver idempotent: send absolute values ("set to 30 s"), or keep a set of ids already applied.

    

  


  

    
Side by side

FitsReadings, metrics, anything replaced soonCommands, payments, anything that must happen

      
Exactly once?Not across a network. At least once plus an idempotent receiver looks like exactly once from outside.
