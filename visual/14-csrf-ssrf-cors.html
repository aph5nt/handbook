Where you meet CSRF and CORS

      
Any cookie session: SameSite, HttpOnly, Secure, always, plus a form token. Any browser API: an explicit list of allowed origins, never * with credentials.

    

    

      
Where you meet SSRF

      
Any feature that fetches a user-given URL: webhooks, link previews, file imports. Resolve the name, block private and metadata ranges, and check every redirect.
