Where you meet joins

      
Facts joined to a small dimension: broadcast it. Two big fact tables: shuffle, or bucket both on the join key so the join is local, as StarRocks does with co-located tables.

    

    

      
Where you meet skew

      
Any group by address on a chain: routers and exchanges are hot keys. Salt them, give them their own path, or let adaptive execution split the big partition.
