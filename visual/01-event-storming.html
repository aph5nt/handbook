Where you meet it

      
A new product: half a day per context, a day for the whole system. A remote team: a shared online board (Miro, FigJam) works, but reading the room is harder. An unclear legacy system: storm what it does today before you replace it. The orange notes often become the event names on Kafka topics, and the clusters become services or modules.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
No session without at least one domain expert. Start with events only, in any order. Mark every argument as a pink hotspot instead of settling it. Draw a border where the words change, not where the database tables are. Leave with a context map, a glossary and a list of unknowns.
