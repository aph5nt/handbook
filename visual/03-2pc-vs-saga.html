Where you meet 2PC

      
Inside one database cluster, or between a message broker and a database that both speak XA. The coordinator is usually hidden in the driver. You pay with locks held across the network, and a coordinator crash leaves every participant stuck.

    

    

      
Where you meet a saga

      
Between independent services that own their own data: order, payment, stock, shipping. Each step commits locally at once. A failure later runs the undo steps backwards: refund, release stock. The system is briefly inconsistent and that is accepted.

    

  


  

    
Side by side

    

      
2PCSaga

      
ConsistencyAtomic. All or nothing at one instantEventual. Correct after compensation finishes

      
LocksHeld from prepare to commit, across the networkNone across services. Each step commits alone

      
Coordinator diesParticipants block, holding locksOrchestrator restarts and resumes from its log

      
UndoRollback by the databaseYou write a compensating action per step

      
FitsOne trust domain, short transactionsMicroservices, long-running business flows

      
Interview line"2PC buys atomicity with blocking. A saga buys availability with compensations. Across service boundaries I choose the saga and design the undo steps first."
