Negative caching: remember that it is not there

Investigators retry addresses with typos, and bots poll fresh addresses with no transactions. Press Next and watch every not found reach the graph. Then switch tabs and cache the absence itself.

Where you meet it

      
DNS caches "no such domain" for the time its server names. Lookup APIs for addresses, users or products that bots probe. It does not stop an attacker who asks for a new random key each time: that needs a Bloom filter.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Cache not found with a TTL much shorter than found answers, such as 30 seconds against 10 minutes. Delete the marker when the thing is created. Never cache an error as not found.
