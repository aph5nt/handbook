Where you meet it

      
Any ordered log or queue: a Kafka partition, an SQS FIFO group, a single TCP connection, HTTP/1.1 pipelining. The more unrelated keys share one ordered line, the more one bad message costs.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Retry a few times in place for short faults, then move the message out: a retry topic or a dead-letter queue. When order per key matters, park the key, not the partition. Libraries such as Confluent's parallel consumer process one partition in per-key lanes.
