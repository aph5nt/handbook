Ingress, network policy and the mesh

Traefik receives api.example, ends TLS and forwards to the API. A NetworkPolicy says only the ingress may talk to the API pods. Press Next to watch a reboot turn the policy on, then open the second tab for the door that bypasses the ingress.

Where you meet it: public hosts

      
Each public host is a Traefik IngressRoute with a certificate from cert-manager. A host-level guard checks that no NodePort is open to the internet.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: policies and the mesh

      
Every service gets a NetworkPolicy that lists the ingress and the scraper. A mesh such as Istio or Linkerd adds mTLS and can send 10 percent of traffic to a new version.
