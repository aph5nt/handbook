Graceful shutdown: drain, then exit

A rolling update replaces a pod of the MCP (Model Context Protocol) proxy. Kubernetes sends the old pod a SIGTERM signal and gives it 30 seconds. Watch it stop taking new queries, finish the ones in flight and exit cleanly. Then watch a pod that just dies. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Every Kubernetes rolling update, scale-down and node drain. A Go server: signal.NotifyContext for SIGTERM, then http.Server.Shutdown with a deadline. A Go indexer that must finish and commit its current batch before it exits, so the next pod resumes from a clean checkpoint.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Catch SIGTERM. Fail readiness first, and wait a few seconds (a preStop sleep) for the balancer to stop sending. Stop accepting, finish in-flight work under a deadline shorter than the grace period, flush and close, then exit 0. Make writes idempotent for the case where SIGKILL still comes.
