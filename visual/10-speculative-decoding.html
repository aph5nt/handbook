Where you meet it

      
Self-hosted serving where decode speed is the bottleneck: vLLM and similar servers support a draft model or extra guessing heads. Hosted APIs often use it out of sight, which is one reason speed varies with the kind of output.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Measure tokens per second on real traffic, with and without it. Pick a draft model trained on text like yours. Expect the biggest win on long, predictable output such as structured JSON, and little or none on hex strings and rare words.
