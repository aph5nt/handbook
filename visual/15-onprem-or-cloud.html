Where you meet it: a data platform

      
The warehouse and graph run on rented bare metal with a terabyte of RAM per server. Kubernetes and open data formats keep a move to cloud possible.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: cloud pieces

      
Stateless workers run on spot nodes with checkpoints; databases run on reserved or metal. Replicas spread across three availability zones (AZs) survive one data-centre loss.
