Where you meet it: a local agent proxy

      
The proxy in front of the graph server is a gateway: it meters every call and holds the free-tier limits. It does not decide what an answer means. That stays in the service behind it.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: Kubernetes

      
Below dozens of services, Kubernetes network policies and a shared client library with retries do the mesh's job. A mesh such as Istio or Linkerd pays off when a platform team runs it for many services.
