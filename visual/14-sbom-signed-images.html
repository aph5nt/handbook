Where you meet it

      
Every image release: SBOM with syft, scan with grype or trivy, sign with cosign, verify with an admission policy. SLSA provenance records which commit and build made it.

    

    

      
Why people ask

      
Incident response: are we affected, answered from the SBOMs. Customers and auditors increasingly ask for SBOMs in contracts.
