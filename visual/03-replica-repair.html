Where you meet it

      
Cassandra keeps hints by default and runs nodetool repair with Merkle trees on a schedule. The original Dynamo paper used sloppy quorum and hinted handoff for an always-writable cart.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Run anti-entropy repair more often than the hint lifetime and the delete grace period. To compare two copies of a large table, compare hashes per range first, then copy only the ranges that differ.
