Where you meet it: indexer, graph sync, oracle

      
The graph sync was a separate service from day one. It ran at a different pace and on a different box. Each of the five services has its own release. A shared read replica would still couple them to the schema, so readers get a published view or events.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: SOA and tracing

      
SOA (service-oriented architecture) is the older cousin: coarse services on a shared bus with one company model. That bus became the new monolith. After any split, tracing comes first, or nobody finds the failing hop.
