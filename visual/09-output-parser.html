Where you meet it

      
LangChain output parsers (JSON, Pydantic), Pydantic validation in plain code, and retry parsers. They matter most with models or providers that have no structured output mode.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Validate against a schema and fail loudly. On a failure, retry once or twice with the error message attached, then stop and report. Never fill a missing value with a default that looks like a real answer. Prefer structured output where the provider has it.
