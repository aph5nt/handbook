Where you meet supervision

      
Akka and Akka.NET, Erlang OTP (Open Telecom Platform) supervisors, Kubernetes restarting a pod. In the IoT backend, a corrupt packet resumed the device actor and a broken connection restarted it. Both rules lived in the parent, not in every handler. Always set a restart limit with backoff.

    

    

      
Where you meet overflow

      
Any actor fed by a plain tell from a faster producer: telemetry, log lines, webhooks. Watch mailbox depth as a metric and alert on it. Bound the mailbox with a rule (drop oldest, drop newest), or move the flow to Akka Streams so the consumer's demand slows the producer.
