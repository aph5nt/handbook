Where you meet it

      
An order in state APPROVAL_PENDING in a microservices order flow: the classic semantic lock. A wallet top-up marked pending until units are granted. Hotel and flight holds that expire after 15 minutes: a semantic lock with a timeout built in.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Add isolation in the data. Set a pending flag with the saga id and a timestamp, make other sagas wait or fail fast when they meet it, and clear it in the final step or the compensation. Run a sweeper that finishes or compensates sagas older than a limit.
