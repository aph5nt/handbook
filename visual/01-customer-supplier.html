Where you meet it

      
The warehouse and the graph sync: a schema change is a planned step, never a surprise. Two teams in one company where the downstream team has a real voice. An internal API with a few known consumers, checked by consumer-driven contract tests (Pact is one tool).

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Write down who is upstream on the context map. Put the customer's contract test in the supplier's build. Change shapes with add, move, drop. If the supplier will never listen, admit it: you are a conformist, or you need a translator.
