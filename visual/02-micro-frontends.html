Micro-frontends

Where you meet it: large web products

      
IKEA, Spotify and Zalando have written about micro-frontends. Tools: Webpack Module Federation, single-spa, or plain links between separately deployed apps.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: the practical choice

      
Split by route, not by widget on one page. Share the design system and the framework as single copies. Keep cross-part communication to the URL and a few events.
