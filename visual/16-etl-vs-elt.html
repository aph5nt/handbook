Where you meet ETL

      
Older tools such as Informatica and SSIS, and any flow where personal data must be masked before it lands. You keep ETL for that one column and load the rest raw.

    

    

      
Where you meet ELT

      
dbt models over Snowflake, BigQuery or StarRocks. Raw tables stay restricted to the pipeline. A chain indexer is ELT in spirit: raw blocks are kept, decoded facts are rebuilt from them.
