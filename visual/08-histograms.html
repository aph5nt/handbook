Where you meet it

      
Chain data: pool, sender and token are skewed by routers and hot wallets. A multi-tenant SaaS table filtered by customer_id. A status column where nearly every row is done.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Collect histograms on skewed columns: ANALYZE TABLE ... UPDATE HISTOGRAM ON col in StarRocks, a higher statistics target in PostgreSQL. Re-collect after big loads. Watch generic cached plans: they cannot use a histogram.
