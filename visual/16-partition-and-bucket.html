Where you meet it: warehouse fact tables

      
Swap and transfer facts in StarRocks: one partition per day, buckets by address hash. Old days move to cheaper disks by partition. A lane reset drops partitions instead of deleting rows.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: everywhere else

      
Kafka partitions by key so one account stays in order. Postgres declarative partitions by month. BigQuery partitions by day and clusters by a column. The choice is the same: time to prune, key to spread.
