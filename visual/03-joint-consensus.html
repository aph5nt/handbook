An etcd cluster of three nodes must grow to five. Press Next to watch two leaders appear when nodes switch member lists one by one, then watch the joint step make that impossible.

Where you meet it

      
etcdctl member add and member remove change one node at a time, through the log. Consul and the HashiCorp Raft library follow the same rule. Growing a three-node graph database cluster to five works the same way.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Always change membership through the cluster's own command, never by editing each node's list by hand. Add one node, wait until it has caught up, then add the next.
