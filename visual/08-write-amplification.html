Where you meet it

      
PostgreSQL hot tables with many indexes: HOT updates skip index writes when no indexed column changes. RocksDB tuning: levelled compaction for reads, tiered for writes. StarRocks: one big load per page instead of many small ones.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Drop indexes nobody reads. Batch writes. Pick the compaction style by the workload. Measure the ratio of disk write bytes to ingest bytes.
