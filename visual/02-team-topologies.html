Where you meet it: Team Topologies

      
Matthew Skelton and Manuel Pais name four team types (stream-aligned, platform, enabling, complicated subsystem) and three interaction modes (collaboration, X-as-a-service, facilitating). Interviewers use these words.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: a reorganisation
