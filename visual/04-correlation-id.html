Where you meet it

      
OpenTelemetry and the W3C traceparent header carry a trace id across HTTP calls and Kafka headers. Many teams also carry a business correlation id, such as the top-up id, in every log line.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Set it once at the edge. Copy it from every incoming message header to every outgoing one and every log line, in shared middleware, so no service has to remember to do it.
