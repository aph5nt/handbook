Where you meet it: re-derivable data

      
An index of a public chain can be rebuilt from the chain, so it accepts a long RPO. Two serving lanes give a short RTO: the idle lane can take over.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: data that exists nowhere else

      
Customer accounts and payments need a short RPO: replication plus point-in-time recovery, an off-site copy, and a monthly restore test.
