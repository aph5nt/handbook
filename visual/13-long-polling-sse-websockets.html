Where you meet it: live feeds

      
A live block or price feed in a browser: SSE. MCP over HTTP streams its replies as SSE too.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: two-way and strict networks

      
A chat with an agent that takes commands mid-stream: WebSockets. A network or proxy that blocks both: long polling.
