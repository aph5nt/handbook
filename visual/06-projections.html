Where you meet projections

      
Akka Projections and Akka.NET persistence queries, Marten, EventStoreDB subscriptions, a Kafka consumer writing a table. In the IoT backend dashboards read projected tables, never the actors. The read model trails the actors by about a second, and that is normal.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Save the offset after the write, or in the same database transaction as the write. Make each update idempotent: an upsert by device id, a "set status to offline", never "add one to the offline count". Then a replay after a crash changes nothing.
