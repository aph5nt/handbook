Where you meet it: a chain warehouse

      
Swap and transfer facts carry keys and amounts. Pools and tokens are dimensions. Address labels are type 2, with valid-from, valid-to and a current flag. Prices sit in their own table by day and are joined at read time.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: any business

      
Orders are facts. Customers, products and stores are dimensions. A customer who moves city is a type 2 change, so last year's sales stay in last year's city.
