Where you meet it

      
PostgreSQL or MySQL read replicas behind a router such as PgBouncer or ProxySQL with a lag limit. Reports and dashboards on replicas, the primary for anything a user just changed. A warehouse that serves reads from replicas of each tablet.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Send heavy or tolerant reads to replicas and must-be-fresh reads to the primary. After a user writes, read from the primary for a short time or wait for the replica to reach the write's position. Watch lag per replica and drop a lagging one from the read pool. When writes are the problem, shard.
