SRE error budgets: how much failure you may spend

The checkout team promises 99.9% of checkout requests succeed over 30 days. That leaves a budget of failure: about 43 minutes of full outage, or the same count of failed requests spread out. Watch a release spend it, the alerts that fire, and the freeze when it runs out. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Google SRE (site reliability engineering) made error budgets the deal between product and operations teams. Most monitoring stacks now ship burn-rate alerts: Prometheus rules, Grafana SLO panels, Datadog and Google Cloud SLO monitors.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Pick a target below 100% that users would notice missing. Alert on burn rate, not on every error: a fast burn pages, a slow burn opens a ticket. Agree the freeze policy in writing before the budget runs out.
