Where you meet Airflow and Dagster

      
Nightly loads, reports and partition moves around a warehouse. Each task takes the logical date and replaces its own partition. Catchup and backfill rerun past dates through the same DAG.

    

    

      
Where you meet Temporal

      
Long business flows with waits, retries and compensations: an order that charges, waits for stock and ships. A chain indexer needs neither for its follow loop: it has its own checkpoint.
