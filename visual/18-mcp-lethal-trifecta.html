Where you meet it

      
A coding agent with a private repo and public issues in one session: outward posts need consent. A research agent that browses the web: no private data in the same session. The term comes from Simon Willison, 2025.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
List each session's servers and check for all three legs together. Remove a leg, or gate the way out with consent.
