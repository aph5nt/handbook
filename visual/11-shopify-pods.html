Where you meet it

      
Pods are cells by another name: the same idea as Amazon's and Uber's cells. Multi-tenant SaaS (software as a service) often shards tenants this way, so one loud customer fills one cell and not the platform.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Default to a modular monolith with enforced borders until one module needs its own scale or its own owner. Splitting early buys network calls and tracing, and nothing else.
