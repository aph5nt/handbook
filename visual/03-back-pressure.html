Back-pressure

An indexer reads blocks at 2,000 rows a second. The warehouse loader behind it writes only 1,000 a second. Press Next to watch an unbounded queue kill the pod, then watch a bounded queue make the indexer slow down.

Where you meet it

      
A Go channel with a buffer size: a send blocks when it is full, which is back-pressure for free. TCP flow control: the receiver advertises a window and the sender never sends more. Kafka: the consumer pulls at its own pace, and lag grows on disk, not in memory.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Give every queue a limit and a rule for the limit: block the producer, slow its rate, or shed work it can drop. Across HTTP the signal is a refusal: 429 Too Many Requests or 503 with a Retry-After header.
