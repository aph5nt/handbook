Where you meet it

      
Cloud storage and disk encryption at rest: S3, GCS and managed disks all use this pattern under the hood. Per-tenant keys let a customer revoke their own data.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
A data key per object or per tenant. A short cache of plain keys, never a log. A regional, monitored KMS, because a KMS outage means no reads.
