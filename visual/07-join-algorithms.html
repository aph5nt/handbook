Where you meet it

      
PostgreSQL loading one customer's orders: a nested loop over an index, a few page reads per row. StarRocks joining one day of swaps to a 30-million-row address table: a hash join that builds on the small day. Two tables both sorted by block height: a merge join that never builds anything.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
You do not choose the algorithm. The planner does, from its row estimates. Your job is to keep statistics fresh, and to check estimated against actual rows in EXPLAIN ANALYZE when a join is slow. A wrong estimate picks a nested loop over millions, or builds the hash on the big side.
