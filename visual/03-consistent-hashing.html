Where you meet it

      
Cassandra and DynamoDB place rows on a ring. CDNs (Content Delivery Networks) and cache clients pick a cache server this way. Kafka uses the simpler hash(key) mod partitions, which is why adding partitions moves keys.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Use a ring with virtual nodes when nodes join and leave often and a moved key is expensive, such as a cache miss. Watch for hot keys: hashing spreads keys, not popularity. Split a hot key with a suffix, or give it its own node.
