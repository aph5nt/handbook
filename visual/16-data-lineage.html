Where you meet it: a re-index

      
Before a lane reset, lineage lists every reader of the tables being dropped: graph layers, rollups, boards. That list is the set of things to stop and rebuild.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: tools

      
dbt builds lineage from its models. Airflow and Spark emit OpenLineage events to a catalog such as Marquez or DataHub. A data contract change starts with a lineage query for its readers.
