Where you meet it: the product's core

      
The warehouse and the graph run on rented bare metal or IaaS, where the team controls hardware, cost and tuning. Managed Kubernetes is an option when the team does not want to run the control plane.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: everything else

      
Functions for glue (a webhook, an upload trigger, a nightly cleanup). SaaS for email, error tracking and anything that is not the product.
