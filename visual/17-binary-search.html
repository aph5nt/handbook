Where you meet binary search

      
Every database index seek. A B-tree on block_height is binary search with wide pages: four page reads for a billion rows. Also git bisect, which halves the commit history to find the commit that broke a test.

    

    

      
Where you meet the scan

      
A table scan reads every row. It wins when you need most of the rows anyway, like a sum over a whole day. It loses badly when you need one row of 21 million: 21 million checks against about 25.
