Where you meet it

      
A Claude Code harness runs claude plugin eval . --runs 3 --ablation with-without after every skill change. Each case runs three times with the skill and three times without it, and the report shows the delta. Code models use pass@k: the chance that at least one of k samples passes a checker.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Hard checks first, in code: is every address 42 characters, is the query text absent. Then a judge model for the soft parts, checked against human labels. A hard rule needs a 100% pass rate. A soft quality rule gets a threshold, such as 90%. Three runs is the floor, more for cases near the line.
