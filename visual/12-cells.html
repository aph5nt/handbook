Cell-based architecture: many small copies of the whole system

A SaaS (software as a service) platform splits its one big stack into cells. Each cell is a full small copy with its own database and its own slice of customers. Watch a bad deploy hurt one cell only, then watch a shared piece undo it all. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Amazon builds many services as cells to cap the blast radius of any one failure. Shopify pods each hold a set of shops with their own database. Slack and others move noisy tenants into cells of their own.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Give each cell the whole stack and share nothing. Fix a maximum cell size and grow by adding cells. Keep the router tiny, cached and redundant. Deploy one cell first, as a canary. Audit every dependency the cells share, because each one is a path for a failure to reach all of them.
