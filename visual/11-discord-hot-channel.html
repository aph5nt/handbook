Where you meet it

      
Any system with a celebrity key: a viral post, a flash-sale product, a popular cache entry. Request coalescing is also called single-flight. Go has it in golang.org/x/sync/singleflight. Put it in the service, above the database.

    

    

      
The same idea at home

      
A busy exchange address in a transfer graph is a hot channel. Many walks touch it at once. Cap its fan-out, cache its neighbourhood, and let one lookup answer many callers.
