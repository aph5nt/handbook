Where you meet mailbox types

      
Akka and Akka.NET mailboxes, chosen per actor or per dispatcher in configuration: unbounded (the default), bounded, unbounded priority, control-aware. The actor's code does not change. Erlang has one mailbox per process and lets a receive pick messages out of order instead.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Alarms, stop and configuration ahead of telemetry: priority or control-aware, for rare urgent messages only. An actor fed by an untrusted fast source: bounded, dropping and counting, never blocking the sender for long. Alert on dead letters and on mailbox depth.
