Where you meet it

      
PostgreSQL holds orders, and a warehouse such as StarRocks, Snowflake or BigQuery holds the copy for reports. A chain indexer reads from the node, StarRocks holds the history for scans, and a graph database serves lookups by address.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Before naming any database, ask: many small requests by key, or few big scans? Keep the two apart and copy between them by CDC (change data capture) or batches. Pick an HTAP engine only when reports must be seconds fresh.
