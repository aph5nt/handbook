Where you meet it

      
Cassandra, ScyllaDB and Riak are leaderless stores in this family. Their consistency levels, such as ONE or QUORUM, are R and W. DynamoDB, the product, kept partitioning but uses a leader per partition and hides conflicts behind conditional writes.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Go leaderless when refusing a write costs more than merging later, like a cart. Use a leader when a conflict would be wrong, like a bank balance. Keep R plus W above N when reads must see the last write.
