Where you meet it

      
QMD over a harness knowledge base: Markdown runbooks split on their headings. Support bots over product docs. Any RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) pipeline, where chunking is the first and cheapest thing to get right.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Split on the document's own structure: headings, then paragraphs. Keep tables and code blocks whole. Put the heading path into every chunk. Split an over-long section with a small overlap and the heading repeated. Test with golden questions: is the right chunk in the top 5?
