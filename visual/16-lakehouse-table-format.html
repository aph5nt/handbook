Where you meet a warehouse

      
Typed facts that people query every day: swaps and transfers in StarRocks, in its own storage. It is fast for small questions and governed by one engine.

    

    

      
Where you meet a lakehouse

      
Raw captures and archives in object storage, as Parquet with an Iceberg table on top. Spark backfills, Trino explores, StarRocks reads it as an external catalog. You expire snapshots and compact files on a schedule.
