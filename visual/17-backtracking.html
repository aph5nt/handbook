Backtracking and pruning

Place four queens on a 4 × 4 board so that no two attack each other: no shared row, column or diagonal. Backtracking places one queen per row, checks at once, and undoes a queen at a dead end.

Where you meet backtracking

      
Sudoku, N-queens, permutations and constraint solvers. A scheduler fitting jobs into slots with rules. A fund-tracing walk with a hop limit, which drops a path once it passes the limit or revisits an address.

    

    

      
The production failure

      
A backtracking regular expression such as (a+)+$ on a long run of as ending in one b tries an exponential number of splits: ReDoS (regular expression denial of service). Use a non-backtracking engine such as Go's RE2.
