Where you meet it

      
Alerts routed by tier, so pages are rare: SLO burn pages, everything else goes to a digest. A rotation and escalation in PagerDuty or Opsgenie. A runbook linked from every alert. A postmortem that ends "liveness no longer calls the database", not "an engineer pushed a bad change".

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
A primary and a secondary each week, with a written hand-off. Escalation after a few minutes without acknowledgement. One incident commander who coordinates and does not debug. Mitigate first: roll back, fail over. Postmortem within days, blameless, with action items that have owners and dates.
