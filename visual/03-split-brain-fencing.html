Where you meet it

      
Kubernetes leader election is a lease object renewed every few seconds. Raft terms, ZooKeeper zxids and etcd revisions are all fencing tokens. Redlock on Redis was shown unsafe because it hands out no token the storage can check.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Write the lease epoch with every batch, and make the warehouse refuse a smaller epoch than the highest it has seen. Never trust the old owner to notice it lost. A process can pause between its check and its write.
