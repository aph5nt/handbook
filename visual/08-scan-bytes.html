Where you meet it

      
BigQuery on-demand pricing bills per byte scanned. Athena and Trino on object storage: bytes read are both the time and the bill. StarRocks: scan bytes per statement shape, next to CPU.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Name columns, filter on the bare partition column, leave wide columns out, dry-run before running, require a partition filter on big tables, and serve repeated dashboards from a materialized view.
