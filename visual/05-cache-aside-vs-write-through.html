Where you meet cache-aside

      
Most read-heavy services: user profiles, product pages, an address-risk lookup in front of a graph database. The app reads Redis, misses, reads the source, and fills Redis with a time to live (TTL). Delete the key on every write, and keep the TTL as the safety net.

    

    

      
Where you meet write-behind

      
Counters, rate limits, page views: values written thousands of times a second. They live in memory and reach the warehouse once a minute in a batch. An actor that holds device state and snapshots later is the same pattern. You accept losing the unflushed window.
