Where you meet it

      
TAO (The Associations and Objects) serves Facebook's social graph from caches in front of sharded MySQL. LinkedIn, Pinterest and X built similar graph caches. Any feed, friend list or "who liked this" screen at scale needs one.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Model the data as objects and typed associations, and cache lists of associations, not raw rows. Accept eventual consistency across regions, but route a user's own reads so they see their writes. Give hot objects extra copies.
