Where you meet it: many services

      
When many teams own many services and every call must be encrypted, Istio or Linkerd gives mTLS, retries, timeouts and per-version metrics with no code change.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: canary by weight

      
The mesh splits traffic between versions by percentage, and its per-version metrics decide whether the new version gets more.
