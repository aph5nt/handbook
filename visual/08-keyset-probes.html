Where you meet it

      
Walking every transfer of a busy address: keyset on (block_height, log_index). A writer finding where it stopped: newest partition, ORDER BY block_height DESC LIMIT 1. A screen with page numbers: offset for the first few pages, keyset for deep walks.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Bound every probe to what the question needs. Keyset for pages, LIMIT 1 for existence and for the newest row. Run anti joins per day partition or per writer page, never over the whole table.
