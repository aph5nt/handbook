Durable workflow: a refund that survives a crash

Ana's trip was cancelled after she paid. Uber must refund her card, wait up to three days for the bank, then email her. Watch a worker crash in the middle and another one replay the history and carry on. Then watch a code change break the replay. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Payouts, refunds, onboarding, order fulfilment: anything that lives minutes to months and has timers. Temporal has SDKs for Go, Java, TypeScript and Python. A coding agent that waits for a review can be a workflow, so a restart resumes it.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
A queue is enough when each step is short and independent. Pick a workflow engine when a process must survive restarts, wait on timers, and never run a step twice. Then keep the workflow code deterministic and version every change.
