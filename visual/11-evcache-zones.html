Where you meet it

      
Redis or memcached as a look-aside cache in front of a database. When losing a zone must not flood the database, keep a full copy per zone and read locally.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Decide per item how stale is acceptable. A row of recommendations can be minutes old, a payment status should not be cached. Warm a new cache copy before it takes traffic, and spread expiry times.
