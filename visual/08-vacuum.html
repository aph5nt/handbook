Where you meet it

      
Hot update tables in PostgreSQL: balances, counters, a job queue kept in a table. StarRocks and LSM stores have no vacuum: compaction drops replaced and deleted rows. The interview probe: why did the table grow when the row count stayed the same?

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Set idle_in_transaction_session_timeout, alert on the oldest open transaction and on forgotten replication slots, and tune autovacuum per hot table. Use VACUUM FULL only in a maintenance window.
