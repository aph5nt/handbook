Where you meet a resolver

      
Every production Akka or Akka.NET cluster with sharding or singletons. Akka's split brain resolver offers keep majority, keep oldest, static quorum, keep referee, down all and lease majority. On Kubernetes, lease majority holds its lock in the Kubernetes API.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Keep majority with an odd number of nodes is the default. Keep oldest suits a cluster singleton, always with down-if-alone. Static quorum only for a fixed cluster size, kept in step as it grows. Down all when being wrong costs more than being down.
