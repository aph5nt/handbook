Dead-letter exchange and redrive

A decode job fails on a RabbitMQ queue and on an Amazon SQS queue. Neither broker has a built-in delayed retry topic. Press Next to see RabbitMQ build one from a time to live and a dead-letter exchange, and SQS move a message aside and back again.

Where you meet the dead-letter exchange

      
RabbitMQ queues set x-dead-letter-exchange. A rejected or expired message is re-published there with an x-death header that counts its trips. One retry queue per delay, each with its own x-message-ttl, gives you backoff.

    

    

      
Where you meet redrive

      
An SQS redrive policy names a DLQ and a maxReceiveCount. SQS moves the message after that many failed receives. A DLQ redrive moves it back to the source queue after the fix ships.
