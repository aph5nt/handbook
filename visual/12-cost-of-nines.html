Where you meet it

      
Two node providers for chain data, so one outage does not stop the indexer. A free analytics tier at three nines and a payment rail at four or more. Cloud providers publish an availability per service: multiply the ones in your request path before you promise anything.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Count every hard dependency in the request path, because each one lowers the ceiling. Turn a hard dependency into a soft one with a cache or a fallback. Add redundancy across real failure domains: other zones, other providers, other deploys. Buy nines only where users pay for them.
