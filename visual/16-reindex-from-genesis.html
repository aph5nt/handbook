Re-index from genesis

A Go indexer walks a chain of 100 days and writes swap facts into StarRocks, one partition per day. A graph sync projects them into DozerDB, and a summary job adds them up. On day 40 a decoder bug is found: days 20 to 40 hold wrong swaps. Press Next and compare patching rows with a full re-index.

Where you meet it: a chain indexer

      
A decoder change means the swap tables must be rebuilt from block 1. The rebuild runs on the staging lane while the other lane serves. Writes are keyed by block height and log index, so a replay overwrites and never duplicates.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: any projection

      
A search index, a cache, a vector store or a graph built from a database. Each one is derived. When the source is fixed, rebuild the derived store from it. Never fix the derived store by hand, because the next rebuild throws the hand fix away.
