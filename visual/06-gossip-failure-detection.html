Where you meet gossip

      
Akka and Akka.NET Cluster, Orleans membership, Cassandra, Consul and Serf all spread membership by gossip. Each node talks to a random peer every second or so, and the news reaches everyone in a few rounds, without a central server.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Tune the acceptable heartbeat pause to the worst pause you have measured, garbage collection and network included. Fix long pauses at their source. Let a split brain resolver or an operator turn unreachable into down, never a timer.
