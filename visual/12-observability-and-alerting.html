Where you meet it

      
Prometheus for metrics with a lane label of two values, Loki or Elasticsearch for logs, OpenTelemetry for traces, Grafana for the dashboard. A postmortem that ends "the health check restarted every copy at once; fix: liveness no longer calls the database", not "engineer X made a mistake".

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Page on symptoms: error rate, latency against the SLO, freshness. Put causes such as CPU, disk and queue depth on the dashboard. Use burn-rate alerts: page when the error budget will be gone in hours, open a ticket when it burns slowly. Keep metric labels few. Put addresses and user ids in logs and traces.
