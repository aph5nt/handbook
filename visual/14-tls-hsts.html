Where you meet TLS

      
Every public endpoint: TLS 1.3, certificates renewed automatically, HSTS on. Inside the cluster, mutual TLS from the mesh.

    

    

      
The client side

      
Go, Python and Node clients: never set skip verify or its cousins outside a local test. Make it impossible in production builds.
