Semi-sync and quorum writes: wait for enough copies

A payment ledger cannot lose a write, and cannot wait for its slowest copy either. Watch semi-sync wait for one replica, then a quorum wait for two of three, and watch both go wrong when the rule is quietly relaxed. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
MySQL semi-sync replication for a primary with a failover replica. Cassandra and DynamoDB style stores, with a consistency level chosen per query. Raft-based stores such as etcd, and a warehouse that confirms a write when a majority of a tablet's three replicas have it.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Use semi-sync when one confirmed copy is enough and alert on every fallback to async. In a quorum store keep W plus R above N for data that must be read fresh, for example N 3, W 2, R 2. Use W 1 or R 1 only for data where an old answer is acceptable, and say so in the design.
