Where you meet it: event-sourced services

      
User profiles and emails in an append-only event log. Events are never rewritten, so personal fields are encrypted per person and the key is deleted on request.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: lakes and warehouses

      
Iceberg and Delta tables: delete rows, then expire snapshots. On a chain, addresses are public, but a person's name on an address is personal data and lives in its own table.
