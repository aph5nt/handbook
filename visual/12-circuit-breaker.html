Circuit breaker

A proxy sends agent queries to graph lane B. Lane B starts failing. With a breaker, the proxy stops calling, answers fast, and probes now and then. Without one, every client retries and keeps the lane down. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Around every remote call that can fail: a database, a node, another service. Netflix wrapped every call with Hystrix, then retired it in 2018 for adaptive concurrency limits, because fixed thresholds needed hand tuning. In Go, a breaker per dependency sits under a timeout and above a connection pool.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
One breaker per dependency and per endpoint, not one per service. A timeout under it, or calls hang instead of failing and the breaker never trips. A fallback that says what is missing. Retries only on top, with backoff and jitter, and only for idempotent calls.
