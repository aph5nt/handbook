Where you meet it: Kubernetes

      
One service account per workload. A Role lists verbs on resources. A NetworkPolicy allow-list per namespace, so a pod reaches only what it names.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: agents

      
A short-lived token with write on the staging namespace only. Production reads use a read-only context. Production writes go through a reviewed workflow.
