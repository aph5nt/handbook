Where you meet it

      
Any read-heavy service: documentation, catalogues, a graph server answering traversals. One big box with a page cache sized to the hot set often beats a cluster of small ones, and it is far simpler to run.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Measure first. Scale up until the biggest box you can buy is not enough, or until users far away need a nearer copy. Horizontal scaling is a choice, not a law.
