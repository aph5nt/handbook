Where you meet it

      
RabbitMQ, SQS, and a Kafka consumer group, where the unit of competition is a partition, not a message. Kubernetes scales the workers on queue depth with KEDA (Kubernetes Event-driven Autoscaling).

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Acknowledge only after the work, so a crash redelivers. Scale until the shared resource behind the workers, often the database, is the limit. When order matters per entity, group by that entity: SQS FIFO message groups, RabbitMQ single active consumer, a Kafka key.
