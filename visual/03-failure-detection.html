Failure detection and failover

A Postgres leader stops answering. Its followers cannot tell if it crashed, is slow, or is cut off. Press Next to watch a safe failover, then watch a timeout that is too short create two leaders.

Where you meet it

      
Patroni for Postgres keeps the leader key in etcd with a time to live and fences through a watchdog. Kubernetes uses node leases and evicts pods after a grace period. A graph database cluster uses Raft heartbeats and has a new leader within seconds.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Make a wrong guess safe first: fence the old leader with STONITH (Shoot The Other Node In The Head) or a storage check. Then tune the timeout for speed. Let a majority, never one node, declare a leader dead.
