Consumer group rebalance

Two graph-sync pods read the four partitions of the transfers topic. A deploy starts a third pod. The group must deal the partitions again. Press Next to see what stops, what is read twice, and how to make it cheap.

Where you meet it

      
Every Kafka consumer group: a deploy, a crash, a scale-up or a long pause starts one. On Kubernetes a rolling deploy can start one per pod. Set partition.assignment.strategy to cooperative-sticky and give each pod a group.instance.id.

    

    

      
What to watch

      
Rebalance count and duration per group, and lag right after a deploy. A group that rebalances every few minutes has a slow consumer or too short a timeout, not a capacity problem.
