Where you meet grains

      
Orleans runs the cloud services of Halo, and suits games, sessions, IoT devices and carts. Akka cluster sharding with passivation gives the same behaviour on classic actors. The runtime keeps one activation per key, so one grain holds one player's state.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Keep grains turn-based, the default, and design calls with no cycles: send what the callee needs in the first call. Use a one-way call when no answer is needed. Mark a method reentrant only when you accept that state may change across an await.
