Stateful workloads

StarRocks backends are a StatefulSet run by an operator. Hot partitions sit on local NVMe; metadata sits on Longhorn. Memory request equals limit.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: protected volumes

      
The runbooks list the warehouse, graph and metrics volumes as protected. A volume whose owner is unknown is protected until proven otherwise.
