SOA and the enterprise service bus

A bank in the 2000s links its account, loan and card systems through an ESB (enterprise service bus). The bus translates every message into one company-wide Customer model and routes it. The integration team owns the bus. Press Next.

Where you meet it: enterprise integration

      
Banks, insurers and telecoms still run ESBs such as MuleSoft or IBM Integration Bus. If you join one, map where the business rules live before changing anything.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: gateways and brokers today

      
A Kafka broker or a RabbitMQ exchange is a dumb pipe. An API gateway must stay one too. The test: could the pipe team change a business outcome? Then it is a bus.
