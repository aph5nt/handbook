Where you meet it

      
Two serving lanes with a pointer that names the live one, kept identical by rule. Two AWS (Amazon Web Services) load balancer target groups, or two Kubernetes deployments behind one service selector. Not for the database itself: data cannot be flipped, only replicated.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Use blue-green where instant rollback is worth double capacity. Keep both sides identical: a difference is a defect. Make every schema change readable by both versions, with expand and contract. Keep the old side running until the new side has served long enough to trust.
