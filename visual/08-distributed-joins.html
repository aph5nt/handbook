Where you meet it

      
StarRocks colocate_with for swaps and pools on pool. Spark spark.sql.autoBroadcastJoinThreshold. BigQuery chooses broadcast or hash joins the same way.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Broadcast a side that stays small. Colocate the pair of tables joined on every query. Otherwise accept shuffle, and watch for a skewed join key.
