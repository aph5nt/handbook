Three-phase commit

Two-phase commit blocks when the coordinator dies after the votes. Three-phase commit adds a pre-commit step so the participants can finish alone. Press Next to see it work after a crash, then fail under a network partition.

Where you meet it

      
Almost nowhere in production. It is an interview question about why 2PC blocks. Spanner removes the blocking another way: it runs 2PC, and each participant and the coordinator is a Paxos group, so no single crash stops it.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Inside one trust domain, make the coordinator fault tolerant by replicating it, rather than adding a phase. Across services, use a saga with compensations instead of any commit protocol.
