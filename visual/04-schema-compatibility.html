Where you meet it

      
Confluent Schema Registry sets a rule per topic: BACKWARD (the default), FORWARD, FULL, or the TRANSITIVE versions that check against every old schema, not just the last. Buf's breaking-change check does the same for Protobuf.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Prefer FULL_TRANSITIVE for topics other teams read and replay: add optional fields with defaults, and nothing else. A breaking change gets a new topic and a migration, never an in-place edit.
