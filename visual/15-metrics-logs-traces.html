Where you meet it: everyday operation

      
Prometheus scrapes every service, Loki stores every log line with lane and service labels, and Grafana draws one board per service with links from a spike to its traces and logs.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: instrumenting code

      
OpenTelemetry in the Go indexer and the graph sync: one span per page and per database call, ids as span attributes, never as metric labels.
