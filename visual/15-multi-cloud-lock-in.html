Where you meet it: the data platform

      
Kubernetes manifests, Kafka and Parquet or Iceberg files keep the warehouse and graph portable. Moving would cost copy time and egress, not a rewrite.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: the rest

      
Managed services for email, CI and error tracking, where lock-in costs little. Multi-cloud only when a contract, a regulator or a unique service demands it.
