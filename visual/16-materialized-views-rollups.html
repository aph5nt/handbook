Where you meet it: a warehouse dashboard

      
StarRocks asynchronous materialized views, refreshed by partition. Daily volume and active-address counts read from the rollup, not from the swap facts.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: everywhere else

      
Postgres materialized views with REFRESH, BigQuery and Snowflake materialized views, a Kafka Streams state store. All trade freshness for speed and need a rule for changed history.
