Where you meet it: a migration latch

      
A service that waits for a minimum database migration at start gets a startup probe. A roll restart on staging proves the start-up check still works before anyone trusts it.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: an API behind an ingress

      
Readiness asks: can I reach my database? Liveness asks only: is my process alive? A dependency never goes into liveness.
