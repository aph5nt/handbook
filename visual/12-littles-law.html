Where you meet it

      
Sizing a database connection pool, a goroutine worker pool in a Go indexer, or Kafka consumer concurrency. Reading a dashboard: if throughput holds and in-flight doubles, latency doubled. It holds for any stable system, whatever the arrival pattern.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Size pools from the p99 time, not the average, plus headroom. Cap concurrency at the pool size and refuse the rest fast. Give calls a timeout, because W without a cap has no upper bound. Watch in-flight count as a metric beside latency.
