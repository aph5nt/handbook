Where you meet it

      
Kafka has no built-in delay, so teams use retry topics with a delay consumer, then a DLQ (dead-letter queue) topic. RabbitMQ uses a TTL (time to live) and a dead-letter exchange. SQS has a redrive policy: after N receives, the message moves to the DLQ.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Three to five retries with backoff and jitter cover a database restart. The DLQ message keeps the error and the original headers and key. The team that owns the consumer owns the DLQ, with an alert on its depth. A replay tool puts messages back in order after the fix.
