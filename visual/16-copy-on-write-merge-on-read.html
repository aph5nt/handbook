Where you meet copy-on-write

      
Daily batch facts in Iceberg or Delta that are rarely corrected. A correction rewrites a few files and every read stays plain.

    

    

      
Where you meet merge-on-read

      
Tables fed by change data capture, with updates all day. Hudi, Delta and Iceberg write delete or log files and compact them on a schedule.
