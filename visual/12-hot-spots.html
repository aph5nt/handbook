Where you meet it

      
An exchange address in a transfer graph: cap the walk, page its links, cache its summary. Discord fixed hot chat channels by coalescing reads above the database. A warehouse partitioned by day: today's partition is hot by design, which is fine for append-only writes.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Measure load per key and per shard, never only the total. Cache hot reads with a short TTL (time to live). Coalesce identical reads in flight into one query. Split a hot write key with a suffix and merge on read. Prefix time-ordered keys with a hash when writes must spread.
