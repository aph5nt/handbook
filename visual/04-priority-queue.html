Priority queue

An email worker has 10,000 newsletter emails waiting when a customer asks for a password reset. In a plain queue the reset waits behind every newsletter. Press Next to see a priority queue let it jump ahead, then see starvation and the Kafka way.

Where you meet it

      
RabbitMQ queues declared with x-max-priority and messages with a priority header. Amazon SQS and Kafka have no message priority, so teams use one queue or topic per priority with its own workers.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Use a few priority levels, not hundreds. Keep prefetch low, because messages already sent to a worker are not reordered. When high-priority traffic is steady, give low priority its own reserved capacity, or it never runs.
