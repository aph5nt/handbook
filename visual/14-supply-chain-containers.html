Where you meet pinning

      
Every lane values file: images by digest only. CI: actions pinned to a commit, secret scanning, dependency alerts. Builds produce an SBOM and a cosign signature.

    

    

      
Where you meet container rules

      
Kubernetes namespaces with the restricted PodSecurity profile. A NetworkPolicy allow-list, inside and outbound. Tests run from the pod where the real caller sits.
