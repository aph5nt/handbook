Where you meet it: lanes and teams

      
One namespace per lane or team, each with a ResourceQuota and a LimitRange. Each workload runs as its own service account.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: production access

      
Agents hold a short-lived staging token. Production reads use a read-only context. Production writes go through a reviewed pipeline. A namespace is not a network wall: pair it with network policy.
