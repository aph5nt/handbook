Where you meet it: a chain indexer

      
A bug found on a lane gets its block range recorded as a sealed fixture. An integration test runs the real binary against a throwaway StarRocks by digest and compares with golden rows. Unit tests cover only pure decoding with no I/O.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: any pipeline

      
A data contract fixes a producer's schema and guarantees for its consumers. Lineage tells you which boards break when a column changes. Freshness and reconciliation alerts watch production.
