Where you meet it

      
Backstage is open source and used far outside Spotify: a portal that lists every service, its owner, its docs and a template for a new one. Any company with hundreds of services needs this map, or new people cannot find anything.

    

    

      
The same idea at home

      
An agent harness needs the same catalogue: an index of skills and runbooks. Agents, like new hires, cannot find what has no map. And one shared event pipe beats each team reading the source on its own.
