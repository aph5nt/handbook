Where you meet it

      
Separate connection pools for the warehouse and the graph inside one Go service. Separate thread pools per dependency in a Java service. At system scale, cells and pods are bulkheads: Amazon cells and Shopify pods each serve a slice of customers, so one bad cell hurts only its slice.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
One pool per dependency, sized from its normal concurrency with some headroom, not a guess. A timeout and a breaker on each pool. A degraded answer that names what is missing, such as a transfer list without USD values when the price service is down.
