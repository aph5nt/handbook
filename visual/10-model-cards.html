Where you meet it

      
Choosing an open or hosted model: Hugging Face model cards and provider system cards. Responsible use reviews, where the card names what the model must not decide alone. Your own adapter or distilled model, which deserves a short card too.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Read the limits and the licence first, the benchmarks second, and decide on your own golden set last. Note the knowledge cutoff and fetch anything newer through RAG (retrieval-augmented generation). Write a card for every model you ship.
