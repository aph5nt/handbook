Where you meet the left rungs

      
A hash map lookup of an address is O(1). A B-tree index seek on block_height is O(log n). One pass over a day of transfers is O(n). A sort before a merge is O(n log n). All four survive millions of rows.

    

    

      
Where you meet the right rungs

      
A nested loop that compares every pool with every other pool is O(n²): fine for a 30-entry registry, never for 1.59 million pools. Trying every subset is O(2ⁿ): only for tiny n, or with memoization and pruning.
