Where you meet SSO

      
Every enterprise customer asks for SAML or OIDC SSO with their own IdP, such as Okta or Entra ID, and often SCIM for accounts.

    

    

      
Which to build

      
New apps: OIDC. It is JSON, simpler, and shares the OAuth machinery. Add SAML when a customer needs it, through a tested library.
