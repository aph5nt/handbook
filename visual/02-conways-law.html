Where you meet it: a platform team

      
A platform team owns the shared rails: the gateway, the pipelines. Not domain services. Inside one codebase, dex asks pricing through one function, not its table: low coupling between owners.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: micro-frontends

      
Micro-frontends split the user interface by team. They work only with a shared shell and a design system, or every team ships a different button. Cohesion: things inside one part belong together. Coupling: what one part must know to change another.
