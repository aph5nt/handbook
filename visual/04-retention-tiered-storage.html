Where you meet it

      
Kafka sets retention.ms and retention.bytes per topic, and deletes whole segments once they are past the limit. Tiered storage (Kafka KIP-405, Confluent, Redpanda, Pulsar) moves old segments to object storage.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Size retention to the longest outage plus the longest replay you will ever run. Set both limits, knowing the first one reached wins. Use tiered storage when history matters and broker disks are expensive.
