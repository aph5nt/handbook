A queue holds 5,000 slow, heavy jobs. Three workers wait. Who decides how many jobs each worker holds at once? Press Next to watch a push with no limit crash a worker, then the same queue with a prefetch limit, then a sync that pulls pages.

Where you meet it in RabbitMQ

      
Set a prefetch count: at most N unacknowledged messages per worker. Prefetch 1 for slow, heavy jobs. Around 100 for tiny messages. With no limit, one worker can take the whole queue.

    

    

      
Where you meet it in Kafka and syncs

      
Kafka consumers pull, so the pace is natural and overload shows up as consumer lag. Alert on lag, not only on errors, and scale consumers up to the partition count. A sync daemon that pulls the next page only after the last one committed gets the same safety.
