Where you meet it

      
Plain tool calls: stateless, scaled by adding replicas. Subscriptions, sampling, long streams: stateful, with sticky routing and resumption by Last-Event-ID. A rolling deploy of a stateful server: drain sessions first.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Start stateless. When a feature needs a session, choose sticky routing for simplicity or a shared store for resilience.
