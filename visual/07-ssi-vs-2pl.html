Serializable: two-phase locking or SSI

Ana and Ben are on call. The rule: at least one stays. Both ask to go off at the same moment. Two engines both promise serializable, and both stop the bug, in opposite ways. Two-phase locking (2PL) makes them wait. Serializable Snapshot Isolation (SSI) lets them run, then aborts one.

Where you meet it

      
PostgreSQL's SERIALIZABLE is SSI. MySQL InnoDB's SERIALIZABLE turns plain reads into locking reads, the 2PL style. CockroachDB is serializable by default, so every client needs a retry loop.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Wrap the whole transaction in a retry loop, not one statement: the retry must re-read and re-decide. Keep transactions short, so fewer pairs overlap. An abort under SSI is normal, and some aborted transactions were in fact safe.
