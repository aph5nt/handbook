Linearizability versus serializability

An airline seat needs every later read to see who booked it. A bank needs a transfer and a report never to mix halfway. Press Next to see why these are two different promises.

Where you meet it

      
etcd and ZooKeeper writes are linearizable, so a lock or a leader name is never stale. Postgres SERIALIZABLE on the leader gives serializable transactions for money moves. Spanner and CockroachDB give strict serializable, using clocks to respect real time.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Ask which promise you need: a fresh single value (linearizable) or no half-mixed transactions (serializable). Never assume a serializable database serves fresh reads from a replica.
