Where you meet it

      
Billing: a wallet with its balance, a ledger of payments beside it. An order with its lines inside, and the customer held by id. A Go indexer: a block aggregate holds its transactions, but the address history lives in the warehouse, never inside the block.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Start from the invariant and put only its data inside. Hold other aggregates by id. Keep unbounded collections outside. Change one aggregate per transaction, and let events carry the rest. If two things must change together at once, merge them on purpose.
