Where you meet it

      
Daily volume per exchange is GROUP BY day, dex. The latest swap per pool is ROW_NUMBER() filtered to 1, instead of a self join. A running balance per address is one sort per partition and one scan.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Pick the tie rule on purpose: RANK leaves gaps (1, 1, 3), DENSE_RANK does not (1, 1, 2), ROW_NUMBER breaks ties in no fixed order. Watch SUM on big integers: StarRocks wraps a BIGINT sum silently instead of raising an error.
