Backpressure: a full queue slows the producer

A Go indexer reads blocks from a node faster than it can write them to StarRocks. Between them sits a queue. Watch a bounded queue fill and push back on the reader, then watch an unbounded queue grow until the process runs out of memory. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
A buffered Go channel between a block reader and a StarRocks writer: a send on a full channel blocks the reader. Reactive Streams and Akka Streams pass demand upstream. Kafka consumers pull only what they can handle. TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) flow control is backpressure on a socket.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Put a bound on every queue. Decide what full means: block the producer (backpressure) when nothing may be lost, drop work (shedding) when stale work is worthless, refuse one client (throttling) when one caller is over its share. Watch queue depth as a metric.
