Window frames, LAG and LEAD: which neighbours a window sees

An analyst wants the 7-day moving average of daily DEX volume, and the change from the day before. A frame tells the window which neighbour rows to look at. LAG reaches one row back. The last tab shows a missing day quietly stretching the frame.

Where you meet it

      
Dashboards: a 7-day moving average of volume. Time between a wallet's transfers: ts - LAG(ts) OVER (PARTITION BY address ORDER BY ts). Sessions: a gap of more than 30 minutes from LAG(ts) starts a new session.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Always write the frame: ROWS BETWEEN 6 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW. Do not trust the default RANGE frame with ties. Before a frame that counts days, join to a calendar table so every day has a row, even a day with zero volume.
