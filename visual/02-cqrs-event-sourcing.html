Where you meet it: money tracing

      
Money tracing and audit are where history is the product. The warehouse answers questions by pool and day. The graph answers questions by address and hop. Both are built from the same chain events.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: a search index

      
A search index or a graph built from a warehouse is CQRS without the name. The practical choice: after every change in its shape, rebuild it from the tables. Use CQRS only when reads differ from writes, or history is the product.
