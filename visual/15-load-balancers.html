Where you meet it: into the cluster

      
A cloud load balancer, or MetalLB on bare metal, is the layer 4 entry. Traefik or nginx ingress pods behind it route by host and path at layer 7.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: inside the cluster

      
gRPC and other long-lived connections need layer 7 balancing (a mesh or a gRPC-aware proxy) or client-side balancing. Sticky sessions only when unavoidable.
