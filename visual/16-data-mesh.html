Where a mesh fits

      
A large bank or retailer with dozens of teams. Each domain publishes data products with owners and contracts, on one platform with a catalog and access control.

    

    

      
Where it does not

      
A startup or a single product team. One warehouse and one owner are simpler. Take the cheap parts anyway: named owners and data contracts.
