Where you meet it

      
Reasoning models think in a separate block before they answer, inside a thinking budget set per call. A Claude Code agent tracing funds across graph hops benefits. A plain lookup or a formatting job does not.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Use a large budget for multi-step questions: graph hops, date maths, comparing rows. Use a small budget or none for lookups. Judge the answer with checks in code, never the written story. Compare pass rate and cost at two budgets and keep the cheaper one that passes.
