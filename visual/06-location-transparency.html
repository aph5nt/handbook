Where you meet it

      
Akka Remote and Akka Cluster, Orleans grains, Erlang distribution: the same send works across machines. In the IoT backend, sharding moved device actors between ingest nodes, and no sender knew or cared.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Design every message as if it crosses a network. Small immutable records, a fixed serializer, no connections or lambdas inside. Assume at most once delivery. Turn on serialize-every-message in tests, and test on a real multi-node cluster.
