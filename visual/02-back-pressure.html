Back-pressure

The graph sync reads pages of rows from StarRocks and writes them into DozerDB. DozerDB writes more slowly than StarRocks reads. Something has to slow the reader down, or the pages pile up. Press Next.

Where you meet it: Kafka and Reactive Streams

      
A Kafka consumer pulls at its own pace, so the broker never floods it. Reactive Streams (Project Reactor, RxJava, Akka Streams) send a demand signal: "give me n". A Go channel with a capacity blocks the sender when full.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: a graph sync

      
Read one page, write it, then read the next. The writer's pace sets the reader's pace, and memory stays at a page or two.
