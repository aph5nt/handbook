Regions and availability zones

A database runs in one region with three availability zones, and keeps a copy in a second region. Press Next to lose a zone and then a region, then open the second tab to see three replicas that were never really apart.

Where you meet it: Kubernetes

      
Nodes in three zones and topology spread constraints on every replicated workload. Zonal disks tie each pod to its zone, so each replica keeps its own disk.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: a second region

      
Only when the business must survive a region loss. It doubles the cost, replication is asynchronous, and a few seconds of writes can be lost.
