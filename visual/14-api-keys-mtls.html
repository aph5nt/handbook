Where you meet an API key

      
Third-party programs with no authorization server: a data feed, a webhook sender, a customer's script. Hashed, scoped, expiring, with two live keys for rotation.

    

    

      
Where you meet mTLS

      
Pod to pod inside a cluster, issued by a service mesh such as Istio or Linkerd, or by cert-manager. Across organisations, OAuth client credentials instead.
