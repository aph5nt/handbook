Where you meet it

      
Ranking RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) chunks by closeness to a question. Finding near-duplicate runbooks or alerts. A vector index in pgvector or StarRocks built with a named distance metric.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Use the metric the embedding model documents. If its vectors should be unit length, normalise them once at write time, then dot product equals cosine and is faster. Tune any "too far to use" threshold on a golden set, never by guess.
