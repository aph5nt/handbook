Where you meet it

      
pgvector, StarRocks and most vector databases offer an HNSW index. It is the default pick for low-latency search over RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) chunks when the vectors fit in memory.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Size RAM for vectors plus graph links. Raise the candidate list setting (often called ef) until recall on the golden set stops improving. Rebuild the index after heavy deletes or updates, and measure recall again.
