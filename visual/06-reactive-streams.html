Where you meet Reactive Streams

      
Akka Streams and Alpakka (Kafka into a database), Project Reactor in Spring WebFlux, RxJava, Java's Flow interfaces. All speak the same four parts: publisher, subscriber, subscription, processor. Go gets the same effect from bounded channels: a full channel makes the sender wait.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Keep a pipeline a stream end to end. Where a stage must hand items to an actor, use one that waits for an acknowledgement, such as Sink.actorRefWithBackpressure, or ask with a parallelism limit. Keep buffers small and explicit, and never hide loss behind a large dropping buffer.
