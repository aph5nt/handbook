Where you meet it

      
Append-only fact tables. A blockchain indexer writes each transfer once and never updates it, and a correction is a new row. Event-sourced systems and audit logs follow the same rule, and they get a change feed for free.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Pick a fixed shard count large enough for years, then move shards between servers instead of rehashing keys. Read by key after a write. Use an index only where a short lag is fine.
