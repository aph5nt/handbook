Where you meet it

      
An indexer conforms to Ethereum JSON-RPC. An app conforms to its payment provider's checkout model, because it cannot change it. Infrastructure code conforms to a cloud provider's resource model.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Conform when the upstream is stable, standard, and out of your hands. Translate when it changes often or its model is messy. Write the choice on the context map, so the next engineer knows it was a decision.
