Top k: quickselect and the heap

A dashboard shows the biggest transfers of the day. Sorting every row is wasted work. Quickselect finds the k-th biggest by partitioning and keeping one side. A min-heap of size k does it in one pass over a stream.

Where you meet top k

      
ORDER BY amount DESC LIMIT 10: the database keeps a top-N heap instead of sorting the table. C++ std::nth_element and NumPy partition are quickselect. A 99th percentile latency over a window is a selection problem.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
A stream, or data that does not fit in memory: the heap. All in memory and you need the k-th value: quickselect with a random pivot. Need everything in order: sort, and only then.
