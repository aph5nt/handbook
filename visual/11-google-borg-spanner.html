Google: one way to run a planet of cheap machines

Google runs on cheap machines, and some fail every hour. Three ideas make that bearable: Borg places every job, Spanner with TrueTime agrees on time across continents, and SRE (Site Reliability Engineering) turns reliability into a budget. Pick a tab and press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Kubernetes is Borg rewritten in public: pods, labels and desired state. Every cluster you deploy to schedules like this, and every pod gets its own IP (Internet Protocol) address because of Borg's port pain.

    

    

      
The same idea at home

      
Pick a clock everyone agrees on. An EVM (Ethereum Virtual Machine) indexer orders by block height, so the chain is the clock and ordering is cheap. Reliability becomes a target and a budget, not a feeling.
