Pushdown: drop rows and columns at the scan

A plan for one day of swaps: a scan, a middle step, and the day filter. Above it, the partitions and columns the scan reads. Watch the filter move down, or get stuck.

Where you meet it

      
StarRocks EXPLAIN: the predicate should appear under the scan node. Spark and Trino reading Parquet: pushdown to row groups decides how many bytes leave storage. A federated query to PostgreSQL: a pushed filter sends a few rows over the network instead of the whole table.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Name the partition column bare, select only the columns you need, and keep filters below windows, LIMITs and outer joins. Check where the predicate sits in the plan.
