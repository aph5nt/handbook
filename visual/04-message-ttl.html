Message time to live

The price API asks a price worker for a price and gives up after 2 seconds. When the workers are backed up, the request sits in the queue long after anyone wants the answer. Press Next to see a time to live drop it, and a time to live on a business command drop orders.

Where you meet it

      
RabbitMQ per-message expiration and per-queue x-message-ttl, with a dead-letter exchange to catch what expires. SQS has a retention period of up to 14 days. Kafka has no per-message expiry: retention is the expiry, and a consumer can skip messages older than its own limit.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Set a time to live on requests that are useless after a deadline: replies, price quotes, login codes. Never let a business command expire silently: dead-letter it and alert.
