Where you meet it

      
Kubernetes pod anti-affinity and topology spread constraints across zones. A database with a replica in another zone and a failover that is tested. Two serving lanes on separate machines: the second lane is the +1 for the whole stack.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
List every single point of failure, including DNS (Domain Name System), secrets, the balancer and the one person who knows the deploy. Size for N, run N+1, or N+2 when maintenance and failure can overlap. Spread copies so the loss of one failure domain still leaves N. Roll config changes gradually.
