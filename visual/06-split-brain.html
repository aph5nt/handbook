Where you meet it

      
Every production Akka or Akka.NET cluster with sharding or singletons. Without a resolver, the same entity runs on both sides and both write one journal. A cluster singleton runs twice the same way.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Always configure a split brain resolver. Keep majority with an odd node count is the default. Never use "auto-down after a timeout": each side downs the other and both keep running.
