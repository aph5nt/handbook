Where you meet it: on every node

      
Log shippers, node metrics exporters and storage agents are DaemonSets. Each gets a memory limit and a careful rolling update, because a bad release hits every node.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: on a clock or once

      
A re-index from block 1 or a schema migration is a Job. Nightly tiering, reports and backups are CronJobs with concurrencyPolicy Forbid and a deadline.
