Where you meet it

      
Two serving lanes kept identical by rule, so either can serve at any time: the parity rule is the disaster recovery plan. Netflix runs Chaos Monkey to kill instances and Chaos Kong to evacuate a whole region, rehearsed until it is a button. On staging, kill the primary database pod and time the failover.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Get RPO (Recovery Point Objective: how much data, in time, you may lose) and RTO (Recovery Time Objective: how long you may be down) from the business. Backups and replication meet the RPO. Failover meets the RTO. Restore backups on a timer. Check DNS, identity and secrets survive the region too.
