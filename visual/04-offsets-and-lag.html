Offsets and consumer lag

The graph-sync group reads partition 1 of the transfers topic. Kafka remembers its place as a number: the committed offset. The gap to the newest message is the lag. Press Next to watch lag grow and shrink, the alert that misses a stuck consumer, and an offset reset that skips data.

Where you meet it

      
kafka-consumer-groups --describe shows each partition's committed offset, end offset and lag. Burrow and the Kafka exporter for Prometheus turn it into metrics. Lag in seconds needs the timestamp of the oldest unprocessed message.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Alert on lag that does not shrink and on lag in seconds, not only on a message count. Set auto.offset.reset=earliest for anything that must not lose data, and alert when a group's offset falls out of retention.
