Where you meet it

      
A risk flag for investigators: high recall floods them with false alarms, high precision misses cases. An LLM (large language model) classifier with labels: precision and recall per label, on a golden set. Retrieval: recall at 5 asks whether the right chunk is in the top 5.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Never report plain accuracy on an unbalanced set. Report precision and recall, per label. Show the trade at a few thresholds, and let the people who pay for each error pick the balance: investigators pay for false alarms, victims pay for misses.
