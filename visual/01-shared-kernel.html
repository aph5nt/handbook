Where you meet it

      
Chain types such as Address, BlockRange and Money shared by the services of one platform team. A client generated from one schema and used by two services of the same team. A shared Protocol Buffers file in a monorepo.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Share only what both sides must agree on exactly. Run both test suites on every kernel change. If the two teams rarely talk, do not share: the joint review becomes a queue. Copy the type instead, or publish a language.
