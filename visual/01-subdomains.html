Where you meet it

      
Chain Insights owns the graph walk and the risk rules, and buys payments and email. A web shop owns pricing and recommendations, and buys the payment provider. A team plan with one budget line per quadrant makes the hiring talk short.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
For each part ask "would we ever outsource this?" A no is core. Keep the core small. For generic parts pick a vendor or an open source project, such as Keycloak for login, and accept its shape. Write your own only where it gives an edge.
