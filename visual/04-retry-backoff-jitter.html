Where you meet it

      
Every client library with retries: the AWS SDKs use exponential backoff with jitter by default, gRPC has a retry policy, Kafka has retry.backoff.ms and retry.backoff.max.ms. Retry topics in a consumer do the same at the message level.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Exponential backoff, full jitter, a cap on the wait, and a cap on attempts. Retry only transient errors and only idempotent operations. Add a circuit breaker when the dependency is down for everyone.
