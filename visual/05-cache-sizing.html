Where you meet it

      
Redis maxmemory: set it from the working set, then watch evictions and hit rate together. A database buffer pool: the hot pages are its working set. A CDN: a few files get most traffic, so a modest edge cache serves most bytes.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Record real keys and replay them at several sizes to draw the miss-ratio curve. Pick the knee, with room above the working set. The percentages in this lesson come from a simple model, not from a real system.
