Where you meet it

      
A token price gets a TTL of one minute. A final block header is cached forever, because it never changes. Negative caching stores "no such address" for a short time, so repeated misses do not hit the source.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Default to a short TTL with a few seconds of random jitter. Put a version of the inputs in the key when the answer must be right after a change. Use event invalidation only when the key is the source id, and keep a TTL under it as a safety net.
