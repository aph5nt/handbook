Join order and join hints: keep every middle result small

A report joins swaps, pools and tokens, filtered to one day of swaps. Watch the bar: it is the size of the middle result after the first join.

Where you meet it

      
StarRocks: JOIN [broadcast] or [shuffle] on a join whose estimate is known to be wrong. PostgreSQL with 10 or more joined tables: past join_collapse_limit it stops searching every order. ORM queries joining many tables: read the order in the plan, not in the code.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Fix the estimate first: collect statistics for the new partition. Use a hint only as a dated, commented exception, and diff the plan whenever data or the engine changes.
