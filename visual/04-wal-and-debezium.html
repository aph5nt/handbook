Write-ahead log and log-based CDC

The order service commits an order and an outbox row in Postgres. Debezium turns that commit into a Kafka message without one line of publishing code. Press Next to see how it reads the database's own log, why that beats polling, and how a stopped connector can fill the database disk.

Where you meet it

      
Debezium on Kafka Connect reads the Postgres WAL through a logical replication slot, the MySQL binlog, or the MongoDB oplog. Its outbox event router turns outbox rows into clean events keyed by aggregate id.

    

    

      
What to watch

      
The replication slot's lag in bytes, from pg_replication_slots. Set max_slot_wal_keep_size so a dead connector cannot fill the disk: the slot is dropped instead, and the connector takes a new snapshot.
