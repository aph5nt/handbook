SKIP LOCKED: a job queue in SQL

Price fetch jobs wait in a PostgreSQL table, and three workers poll it. With plain FOR UPDATE they queue behind the same first job. With SKIP LOCKED each takes a different job. The last tab shows the failure: a transaction held open while a long job runs.

Where you meet it

      
PostgreSQL job queues: River (Go) and good_job (Ruby) are built on SKIP LOCKED. MySQL 8 supports it too. It suits a few thousand jobs a minute with workers that already talk to the database.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Claim a job in a short transaction, set status = 'running' and a claimed_until time, and commit before the work starts. A reaper resets claims that ran out. Make every job idempotent. For high volume, or many consumers of one event, move to Kafka or a real queue.
