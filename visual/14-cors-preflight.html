Where you meet preflights

      
Any API called from a browser page on another origin: a partner dashboard, a single-page app on a different host, a browser extension.

    

    

      
What it is not

      
A preflight is not CSRF protection. A simple form POST skips it entirely, and the cookie still rides along. Use SameSite and form tokens for that.
