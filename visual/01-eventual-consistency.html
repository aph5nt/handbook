Where you meet it

      
An indexer and a graph: the warehouse has a swap at block N, the graph shows it a minute later. A search index behind a database. DNS, where a change spreads over minutes. Read replicas in Postgres, where a read right after a write can miss it.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Ask the business how long a window is acceptable, then measure it. Show pending states and freshness in the interface. Return the command result to the caller so they read their own write. Never trigger an irreversible action from a read that may be stale.
