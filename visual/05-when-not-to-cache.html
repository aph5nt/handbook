Where you meet it

      
The chain head height changes every tenth of a second, and every reader wants the latest: never cache it. A balance before a transfer, or stock before a sale, must be exact: read the source.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Walk the four questions before building a cache. Fix a slow query or add an index first. If you do cache, measure the hit rate, the source load and the tail latency. Below about 80 percent hits, the cache may cost more than it saves.
