Where you meet it: fund tracing

      
A sync reads transfer facts from StarRocks and merges FLOWS_TO links into DozerDB. Graph search walks outward from one address. Model-derived labels such as scam or victim are a separate layer with their own rebuild.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: any derived store

      
A search index, a cache or a vector store built from a database. Each is rebuilt from the source. Writes go to the source, and the projection follows.
