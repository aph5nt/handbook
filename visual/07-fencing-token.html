Fencing tokens: refuse the stale writer

Two indexer pods share one lane, and only one may write. Pod A holds the lease with epoch 7, then freezes in a long garbage collection pause. Watch pod B take over with epoch 8, and watch the checkpoint row refuse pod A's late write. The second tab replays it with no token.

Where you meet it

      
An indexer writer lease, with the epoch carried on every checkpoint write. Kafka: a transactional producer's epoch fences an old "zombie" producer. etcd and ZooKeeper: the revision number of a lock serves as the token.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Take the lease in one statement that bumps the epoch. Put AND epoch <= :e on every write the writer makes. Treat zero rows changed as "you are no longer the writer" and stop. Use the database clock for expiry, never the pod's clock.
