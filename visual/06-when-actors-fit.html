Where actors fit

      
One actor per device, session, cart, connection or workflow instance. In the IoT backend only the device-state service of four services held actors. Cross-device dashboards read the warehouse, never a thousand actors.

    

    

      
Where they do not

      
A Go indexer that fetches blocks, decodes them and writes batches to StarRocks. Bounded Go channels give it back-pressure: a full channel makes the fetcher wait. CRUD services, reports and batch jobs: the database or a stream is the better tool.
