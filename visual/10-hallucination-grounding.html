Where you meet it

      
A Chain Insights answer: every address comes from a query result, never from memory. RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) over runbooks, where each answer cites the chunk it came from. Structured output with enums, so a field cannot hold an invented value.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Give the facts with their source. Return identifiers in typed fields that code can check against the store. Allow "not found" as a good answer. Put negative cases in the eval set, and log misses in production. A bigger model hallucinates less, not never.
