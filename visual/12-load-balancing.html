Where you meet it

      
Traefik or NGINX as an L7 (application layer) ingress routing by path. An L4 (transport layer) load balancer in front of a TCP (Transmission Control Protocol) service such as a database proxy. gRPC (Google remote procedure call) clients that balance per request on the client side because one HTTP/2 connection carries every call.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Round robin when every request costs the same. Least connections or power of two choices when cost varies, as with graph walks. Weighted when copies differ in size. Hash of a key when a copy keeps a cache for that key, and never for a key that can be hot. L7 for long-lived HTTP/2 connections.
