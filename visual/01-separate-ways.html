Where you meet it

      
Billing and the graph at Chain Insights: no link. Two teams that each need a small date or address parser: copy it, do not share it. Any integration whose only reason is that the other team already has the code.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
For each possible link, write the gain and the coupling side by side. When the gain is small, build nothing and accept a little duplication. Mark it on the context map as separate ways, so nobody adds the link later by accident.
