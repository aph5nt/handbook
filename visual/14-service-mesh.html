Where you meet a mesh

      
Many services owned by many teams: Istio, Linkerd, or a node-level mesh. One identity, policy, retry and metrics layer for all of them.

    

    

      
When it is too much

      
A handful of services: NetworkPolicy plus cert-manager may be enough. A mesh adds latency, memory per pod, and one more system to run.
