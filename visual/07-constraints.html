Where you meet it

      
An app database: orders and lines with a foreign key and RESTRICT. A wallet: CHECK (balance >= 0) as the last guard behind the conditional UPDATE. A warehouse such as StarRocks: foreign keys are not enforced, so an orphan count runs after every load.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Put the rule in the schema when every writer must obey it. Use RESTRICT by default. Use CASCADE only for small, owned children, and delete large sets in batches on purpose. In a warehouse, check orphans with a LEFT JOIN to the parent WHERE parent.key IS NULL.
