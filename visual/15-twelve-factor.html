Where you meet it: every Kubernetes service

      
Settings come from ConfigMaps and Secrets, logs go to stdout, state goes to StarRocks or the graph. The image is the same on every lane.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: a graceful stop

      
On SIGTERM the indexer finishes the current page, saves its checkpoint and exits before the grace period ends. Its writes are safe to repeat, so a hard kill costs one page.
