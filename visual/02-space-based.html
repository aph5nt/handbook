Space-based architecture

A concert sale opens at 10:00. Thousands of buyers hit a seat map of a few thousand seats. Processing units keep the seat map in memory, copy changes to each other, and write to the database later. Press Next.

Where you meet it: ticketing and auctions

      
Concert sales, flash sales and online auctions: a short, huge burst on a small set of data. Hazelcast, Apache Ignite and GigaSpaces (where the name comes from, a tuple space) provide the grid.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: when not to

      
A chain indexer moves terabytes of history. That cannot sit in memory. Space-based fits only when the hot data is small and the burst is short.
