Where you meet it

      
Airbnb moved from the Monorail to SOA (service-oriented architecture) from about 2017, then grouped services into blocks. Uber's DOMA (domain-oriented microservice architecture) and its gateways solve the same "too many services" problem.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Pull out the data a monolith reads most, run the new service beside the old code on real traffic and compare, then switch. When product teams have to call a dozen services, give each business area one facade API.
