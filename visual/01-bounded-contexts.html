Where you meet it

      
A blockchain indexer calls a thing an "address". The risk team calls the same thing a "wallet" with a label and a score. Billing calls it a "subscriber". One Go struct for all three grows a field per team until nobody dares change it. Three contexts with their own types, joined by ids and events, keep each one small.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Put a border where the language changes, which is usually where the team changes. Share ids, never objects. Wrap anything you do not own (a legacy table, a vendor API) in a translator so its names stop at the border.
