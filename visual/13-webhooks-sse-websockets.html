Where you meet it: payments and events between companies

      
Stripe, GitHub and most payment providers send signed webhooks with an event id, and retry on anything but 2xx. Offer a polling endpoint as well: every client understands it.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: live views

      
A live view of new blocks in a browser: SSE. A chat with an agent that also takes commands: WebSockets. A server-to-server event: a webhook.
