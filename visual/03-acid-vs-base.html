Where you meet ACID

      
A bank ledger inside one database, always. Postgres or MySQL give Atomic, Consistent, Isolated, Durable transactions. An analytics warehouse such as StarRocks makes each batch load atomic.

    

    

      
Where you meet BASE

      
An order flow across payment, stock and shipping: a saga plus an outbox. Reports on such data are eventually consistent with the source. Mark in-between states as pending so nobody mistakes them for final.
