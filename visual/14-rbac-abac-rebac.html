Where you meet RBAC

      
Kubernetes and most admin tools: a Role lists verbs on resources, a RoleBinding gives it to a service account.

    

    

      
Where you meet ABAC and ReBAC

      
ABAC for tenant and region rules taken from the token. ReBAC for sharing, such as cases assigned to teams, with OpenFGA or SpiceDB.
