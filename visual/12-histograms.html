Where you meet it

      
Prometheus histograms with an le (less or equal) label per bucket: sum the buckets across copies, then call histogram_quantile. HDR histograms and t-digests do the same with finer buckets. A load-test tool that reports per-worker p99 and then averages them has the same bug.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Record a histogram, not a precomputed percentile. Put bucket edges close to the numbers you alert on, such as the 300 ms SLO line, because a percentile is only as exact as its bucket. Merge across copies and time by adding counts. Never average percentiles.
