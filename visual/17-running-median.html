Running median with two heaps

A gas price monitor shows the median fee of everything seen so far, updated with every new transaction. The smaller half lives in a max-heap, the bigger half in a min-heap. The median waits at their tops.

Where you meet it

      
A live median of fees, latencies or trade sizes on a dashboard. Percentile monitors use the same split at 95% or 99%. The interview question "find the median from a data stream" is this design.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Rebalance after every insert. Over a sliding window, old values must leave too: mark them removed and drop them when they reach a top, or keep the window in an ordered tree.

    

  


  

    
Side by side

    

      
Sort on every valueTwo heaps

      
InsertO(n log n), or O(n) into a sorted listO(log n)

      
Read the medianO(1) after sortingO(1)

Interview line"Keep the smaller half in a max-heap and the bigger half in a min-heap, rebalance so their sizes differ by at most one, and the median is always at the tops: O(log n) per value, O(1) to read."
