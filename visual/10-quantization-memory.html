Where you meet it

      
Self-hosting with vLLM or llama.cpp on on-premises GPUs (graphics processing units), where cards are the budget. A small router model at 8-bit for a narrow task. A LoRA adapter trained on top of a frozen 4-bit base.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Size for weights plus KV cache at peak users, never weights alone. Start at 8-bit, which is nearly lossless. Go to 4-bit only after your own golden set says the loss is fine. A small model at 8-bit often beats a big model at 4-bit on a narrow task.
