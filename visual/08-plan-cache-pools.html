Where you meet it

      
The Go indexer's page write: one prepared statement, the parse paid once. A query by pool or by customer where a few values are huge: the plan built for a small value is reused for a big one. Fifty pods with pools of 20 against one StarRocks front end: 1,000 connections and their prepared statements.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Give the planner histograms on skewed columns, or keep a plan per class of value. Size pools by the multiplication: pods times pool near the database's cores times a small factor. Put a shared pooler in front when pods scale, and close prepared statements you no longer use.
