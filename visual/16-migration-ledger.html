Where you meet it: a warehouse family

      
One runner per database family, recording ids and checksums in a ledger table. Services wait for a minimum id at start. Migrations are tested against a throwaway engine, from empty and from the previous version.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: any app database

      
Flyway and Liquibase keep the same kind of ledger with checksums. On a hot table you cannot lock, create the new shape beside it, backfill, switch readers, then drop the old.
