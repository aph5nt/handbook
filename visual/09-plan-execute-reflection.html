Plan and execute, then reflect

A change needs eight tasks. One strong model writes the plan. Cheaper executor agents take one task each. Task 3 fails because a table is named differently, so the planner rewrites what follows. At the end a critic reads the finished branch. Then switch tabs to watch a critic approve its own mistake.

Where you meet it

      
An OpenSpec change in an agent harness: plan once, one executor per task, a reviewer at the end. A skill improvement loop: run 100 real conversations, grade each against the rules, fix the skill, run again. Any long task where re-thinking every step with ReAct would be slow and costly.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Spend the strong model on the plan and the review. Spend the cheap model on the steps. Check after every step and re-plan at once, not at the end. Give the critic a fresh context, its own prompt, a rubric and a golden answer when one exists. Cap the rounds.
