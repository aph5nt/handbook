A* search

A delivery robot crosses a warehouse floor with a wall in the middle. Dijkstra spreads out evenly in every direction. A* adds a guess of the distance left and heads for the goal. Same floor, same shortest path, far fewer cells searched.

Where you meet A*

      
Route planning in maps and games, robot path planning, and puzzle solvers where the guess counts misplaced tiles. A fund-tracing search toward a known target can rank its frontier by an estimate of hops left.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Pick a guess that never overestimates: on a grid with four moves, the Manhattan distance, rows apart plus columns apart. A guess of 0 is safe but no faster than Dijkstra. A guess that is too high is faster and can be wrong.
