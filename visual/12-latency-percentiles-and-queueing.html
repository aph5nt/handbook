Where you meet it

      
Every latency dashboard and every SLO (Service Level Objective). Prometheus histograms let you compute p50, p95 and p99 and merge them across hosts. A p99 of p99s is not a p99, so never average percentiles: merge the histograms.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Report p50, p95 and p99, never the mean alone. Set timeouts and budgets on p99. Keep steady utilisation at 50 to 70 percent so a spike has room. Little's law sizes the pool: 100 requests per second at 0.2 seconds each means 20 in flight.
