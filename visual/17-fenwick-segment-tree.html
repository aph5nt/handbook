Fenwick tree and segment tree

The block-volume dashboard gets late corrections all day. Prefix sums make every correction rewrite all later totals. These two trees answer a range sum and take a correction in O(log n) each. Same eight blocks as the Prefix sums lesson.

Where you meet them

      
Live range sums with constant corrections: volume per block range, order book depth per price band. Rank queries such as "how many wallets trade less than mine?" are a Fenwick tree over volume buckets.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Only sums or counts: a Fenwick tree, one array, short code. Min, max, or adding to whole ranges: a segment tree, with lazy propagation for range adds. A Fenwick tree counts from 1.
