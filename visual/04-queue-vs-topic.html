Where you meet a queue

      
RabbitMQ or Amazon SQS (Simple Queue Service) for task queues: resize an image, send an email, re-decode block N. Add workers to go faster. If order matters per account, route one account to one worker.

    

    

      
Where you meet a topic

      
Kafka or Amazon SNS (Simple Notification Service) for events many teams read. The publisher never learns who reads. So removing a field can break a reader nobody knew about. A schema registry guards against that.
