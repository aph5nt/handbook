Where you meet it

      
Every consumer meets one eventually: a producer release with a renamed field, a value out of range, a message too big to parse. Spring Kafka's error handler lets you list exceptions that skip retries and go straight to the dead-letter topic.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Treat parse and validation errors as permanent: park them at once with the error. Treat timeouts and 5xx answers as transient: retry with backoff. When a dependency is down for everyone, pause consumption instead of parking every message.
