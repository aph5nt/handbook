Where you meet them: stream processors

      
Flink, Kafka Streams and Spark Structured Streaming all offer the three types. You pick tumbling for totals, sliding for rolling numbers, session for bursts per user, and you set a watermark and a late-data rule for each.

    

    

      
Where you meet them: a chain warehouse

      
Daily swap volume is a tumbling window in SQL: GROUP BY day. A rolling one-hour price is a sliding window. A trader's burst of swaps, the shape of a bot or a sniper, is a session window per address.
