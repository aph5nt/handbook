Where you meet it

      
Customer support or case notes sent to a hosted model API. Eval sets built from production traces, which must be scrubbed before they go into git. Public blockchain data needs no redaction: it is already public, but a wallet linked to a name is PII.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Classify the data first. Route everything that leaves (prompts, tool results, traces, eval sets) through one redactor in code. Keep the placeholder mapping inside. Where text cannot be redacted, use a no-retention contract or a model on your own hardware. Log what was sent.
