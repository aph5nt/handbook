What consensus cannot do: FLP and Byzantine faults

Two famous limits sit under every consensus system. With no clock, a silent node may be dead or just slow. And Raft trusts every node to tell the truth. Press Next to see both limits, and what real systems do about them.

Where you meet it

      
etcd, Consul and a graph database cluster assume crash faults only and run Raft inside one trust domain. Ethereum proof of stake and Tendermint-based chains are Byzantine fault tolerant, and need two thirds of the stake to finalise a block.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Inside one company, use Raft with 2f + 1 nodes and timeouts. Between parties that do not trust each other, use a BFT (Byzantine fault tolerance) protocol with 3f + 1 nodes and signed messages, or a public chain.
