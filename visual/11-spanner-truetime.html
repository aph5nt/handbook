Spanner TrueTime: wait out the clock doubt

Ana sends 100 euros from Frankfurt. Ben checks the balance from Iowa a moment later. Spanner gives every commit a timestamp, and it promises that a later commit always gets a later timestamp, across the world. Watch how it keeps that promise by waiting out its own clock doubt. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Google Spanner runs AdWords and Google Play. Cloud Spanner sells the same design. CockroachDB and YugabyteDB copy the idea without atomic clocks: they use a wider error bound and fix ordering with retries instead of a long wait.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Pay a few milliseconds per write for global order and lock-free reads at any past time. Choose it when a bank, a ledger or an ad budget must be correct across regions. Skip it when one region or eventual consistency is enough.
