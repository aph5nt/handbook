Where you meet it

      
A columnar warehouse such as StarRocks keeps three replicas per tablet and confirms a write by majority. PostgreSQL and MySQL run async read replicas for dashboards. Kafka waits for the in-sync replicas when the producer asks for all acknowledgements.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Send dashboards and reports to read replicas. Send anything a user just changed to the primary for a short time, or wait until the replica has reached the write's position. Before you fail over to an async replica, decide how many seconds of writes you are allowed to lose.
