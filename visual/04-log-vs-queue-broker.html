Log broker versus queue broker

A team adds a new graph layer and must build it from a year of events. On a log broker the events are still there. On a queue broker they were deleted when the first reader acknowledged them. Press Next on each tab.

Where you meet a log broker

      
Kafka, Pulsar, Redpanda: event streams that many readers follow, and that a new reader may replay. A compacted token_metadata topic keyed by contract address gives a new consumer the current name and decimals of every token in one read.

    

    

      
Where you meet a queue broker

      
RabbitMQ, Amazon SQS, NATS core: task queues, rich routing with topic, header and fanout exchanges, and request-reply. Do not pick Kafka for ten messages a second. Running it costs more than the problem.

Interview line"A log keeps history and lets every reader keep its own place. A queue forgets on acknowledgement. I pick the log when replay matters."
