Where you meet it

      
Connection actors: connecting, online, reconnecting. Any actor with a start-up phase: loading state, waiting for a lease, recovering events. Akka Become and Stash, Akka Typed behaviours, Erlang gen_statem.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
One behaviour per state, each handling only what makes sense there. Stash what arrives too early, give the stash a capacity, put a timeout on every waiting state, and unstash on every switch to the working state.
