Where you meet it

      
Very large collections where memory is the limit, often with compressed vectors. FAISS (a vector search library) and many vector databases offer IVF. Pairing it with compression keeps a billion vectors on one server.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Raise the number of probes (often called nprobe) until recall on the golden set is good enough, then stop: each probe costs time. Retrain the centroids when the data shifts a lot. Pick HNSW instead when the vectors and links fit in memory.
