Choreography versus orchestration

A customer orders a phone. Payment, stock and shipping must each do their part. Either each service reacts to the others' events, or one order process tells each step what to do. Press Next.

Where you meet it: events between teams

      
Choreography suits a few steps between teams that should not know each other: a new reader of OrderPlaced costs Orders nothing. Add a timeout watcher so a stuck order is noticed.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: workflow engines

      
Temporal, AWS Step Functions and Camunda are orchestrators. A lane reset that stops every writer in order is orchestration too: one runbook owns the sequence.
