Where you meet it

      
Every production client of a hosted model API. Providers limit requests per minute and tokens per minute, and return 429 with a retry-after time. An agent harness adds a per-session token budget, so one runaway loop cannot eat the account's limit.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Retry with exponential backoff plus random jitter, honour retry-after, and cap the retries. Then fall back to another region or model chosen in advance and tested on the golden set. Make requests idempotent, so a retry never repeats a side effect. Plan capacity by tokens per minute at peak.
