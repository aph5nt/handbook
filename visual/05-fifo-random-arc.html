Where you meet it

      
Redis offers allkeys-random beside approximated LRU and LFU. ZFS caches files with ARC. Caffeine in Java uses W-TinyLFU, another scan-resistant policy. Postgres reads big scans through a small ring of buffers so they cannot flush the buffer pool.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
LRU is the default. Pick random when reads are spread evenly and every nanosecond counts. Pick a scan-resistant policy, such as ARC or W-TinyLFU, when reports or backups scan the same data the live reads use.
