Where you meet CDC

      
Debezium reading the Postgres write-ahead log or the MySQL binlog into Kafka, then into a warehouse, a search index or a cache. You watch the replication slot lag and upsert by key.

    

    

      
Where you meet it in patterns

      
The outbox pattern: a service writes events to an outbox table in the same transaction, and CDC publishes them. A chain indexer is CDC on a blockchain: the chain is the log, the block height the position.
