Where you meet it: a gateway

      
A token bucket per caller and per tier, with the counters in a shared store. A concurrency limit, how many requests at once, sits beside it for slow backends.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: your own outbound client

      
Calling someone else's API, run the same bucket on your side, so you never hit their 429.
