Cell-based architecture

An API serves thousands of tenants. It is split into cells: each cell is a full copy of the stack with its own database, serving a slice of the tenants. A thin router sends each tenant to its cell. Press Next.

Where you meet it: large SaaS

      
AWS builds many services from cells. Slack and DoorDash have written about moving to cells to limit the blast radius of a bad deploy or a bad tenant.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: two lanes and a pointer

      
Two full lanes with a pointer that names the serving one is the two-cell cousin: used for switching versions, not for splitting tenants. The same rule holds: nothing shared between them that can take both down.
