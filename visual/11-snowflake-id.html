Where you meet it

      
Primary keys read in time order. ULID (Universally Unique Lexicographically Sortable Identifier) and UUIDv7 follow the same idea: time first. New rows land at the end of the index, while random UUIDs scatter inserts everywhere.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Hand out machine ids from one place, such as ZooKeeper, and check them at start-up. A blockchain indexer needs none of this: block height and log index are already a total order.
