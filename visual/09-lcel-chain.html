Chains, runnables and LCEL

In LangChain every step is a runnable: something you can invoke. A prompt template, a model and an output parser are joined with a pipe into a chain, and the chain is a runnable too. Where you meet it

      
LangChain's LCEL (LangChain Expression Language) and its Runnable interface. The same idea is a pipeline of functions in plain code. LangGraph builds on runnables when a chain needs loops and state.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Use LCEL when you want batch, streaming, retries and tracing for free on a short fixed pipeline. Turn on tracing so every step shows its input and output. For a flow of two or three calls, plain functions are just as good and easier to read.
