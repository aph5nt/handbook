LinkedIn: one log instead of a web of feeds

Profile edits, member activity and job posts must reach search, recommendations and Hadoop. First watch every pair get its own feed and one feed break quietly. Then watch Kafka turn the web into one log. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Kafka, Redpanda, Pulsar, Kinesis: one durable ordered log, many readers with their own offsets. Change data capture tools such as Debezium stream a database's changes into it, so the database stays the truth and the log carries the news.

    

    

      
The same idea at home

      
An indexer writes chain facts to the warehouse. The graph sync and every other reader read the warehouse later, at their own pace. The warehouse table plays the log. No reader reaches the chain node directly.
