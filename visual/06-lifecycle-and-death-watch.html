Where you meet it

      
Session and subscription actors that watch the entity they show. A parent that must react when a child stops for good, not only when it fails. Akka Context.Watch and Terminated, Erlang monitors (and links, the stricter form: one dies, both die).

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Open resources in PreStart and close them in PostStop, never in the constructor. Handle Terminated in every behaviour of a watcher. Use supervision for a parent's decision about a failure, death watch for anyone who needs to know an actor is gone.
