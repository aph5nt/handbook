WhatsApp: a relay, not an archive

Ana sends Ben "See you at 8". The server keeps the message only until Ben's phone takes it, then forgets it. Watch why that let a few dozen engineers serve hundreds of millions of people, and what it cost later. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Erlang and Elixir run chat, telecom switches and message brokers like RabbitMQ. Akka and Akka.NET bring the same actor model to the JVM (Java Virtual Machine) and .NET: an IoT (Internet of Things) device becomes one actor, and the runtime handles the hard concurrency.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Decide what you will not store. A relay that forgets after delivery removes most of the storage problem. The price is paid later, when a feature needs the data you chose not to keep.
