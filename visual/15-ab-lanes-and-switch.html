A/B lanes and the switch

A blockchain analytics platform must rebuild its swap tables from block 1 without a customer ever seeing a half-built table. It runs two identical lanes and a pointer that names the one serving customers. Press Next and watch a release, then watch what drift between the lanes does.

Where you meet it: a whole data platform

      
Each lane has its own StarRocks warehouse, DozerDB graph and API on Kubernetes. A re-index from block 1 runs on the staging lane for hours. The switch is a merged pull request with the operator as required reviewer, so git history is the audit log.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: a plain web service

      
Blue-green: two Deployments, and the Service selector or the ingress names the live one. Cheaper still is a canary: one pod of the new version first, then 10, 50, 100 percent while the error rate is watched.
