Where you meet it

      
Akka OneForOneStrategy and AllForOneStrategy. Erlang OTP supervisors: one_for_one, one_for_all, rest_for_one. Entity actors (devices, sessions, carts) use one-for-one, the default.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Ask: do the children share anything? Independent entities get one-for-one. Children that share a connection, a file or a protocol state get all-for-one. Erlang's rest-for-one fits a chain where later children depend on earlier ones. Always set a restart limit.
