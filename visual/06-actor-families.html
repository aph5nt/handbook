Where you meet classic actors

      
Erlang OTP (Open Telecom Platform) runs telephone switches, WhatsApp and RabbitMQ. Its gen_server is the standard actor: call is ask, cast is tell. Akka and Akka.NET copied the model with a supervision tree. Pick them when failure handling is part of the domain.

    

    

      
Where you meet virtual actors

      
Orleans grains, and Akka cluster sharding when it starts entities on demand. Games, sessions, carts, simple entity state. Less code: no tree, no create, no stop. The price: a failure is just an exception at the caller.
