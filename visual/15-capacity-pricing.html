Where you meet it: always-on systems

      
Databases, the warehouse and the graph run on reserved capacity or rented bare metal, sized from the measured floor.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: interruptible work

      
Batch jobs, CI runners and stateless workers run on spot with checkpoints, short tasks and an on-demand fallback pool. A monthly cost review covers each service, with egress as its own line.
