Where you meet it

      
A legacy monolith with a shared models package and a common database. The strangler fig pattern: new code beside the old, traffic moved one route at a time. A review where someone adds a shared common table: that is the first grain of mud.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Draw the contexts on paper first. Cut out the one that changes most, behind a translator. Ship after every cut. Leave the rest alone until its turn. Refuse new shared types without an owner.
