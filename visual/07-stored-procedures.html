Stored procedures: one call, and its price

Moving money between two accounts takes five statements. Run them from the app and you pay five round trips with locks held. Run them in a stored procedure and you pay one. The second tab shows the bill that arrives years later.

Where you meet it

      
Banks and older enterprise systems: Oracle PL/SQL and SQL Server T-SQL hold years of business rules. PostgreSQL: a function for one atomic operation that several services share. Most new systems keep logic in the app.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Keep a procedure small and data-shaped: one atomic multi-row write, or a permission boundary. Version it in migrations and test it against a real database. Business rules such as fees and limits stay in the app, where they are tested, logged and traced.
