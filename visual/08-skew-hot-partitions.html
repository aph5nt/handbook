Where you meet it

      
Router contracts and exchange hot wallets in chain data: always skewed. Kafka topics keyed by customer, where one customer is huge: the same problem, one hot partition. A time-partitioned warehouse such as StarRocks: today is always the hot partition.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Broadcast the small side instead of shuffling on a skewed key. Split a heavy key with a salt when both sides are big. Give today's partition more buckets, or add a second partition dimension.
