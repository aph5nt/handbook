SLO and the error budget: reliability as a number

A blockchain data product promises fresh data. The team measures how far behind the chain head the data is, sets a target, and treats the allowed misses as a budget. Watch a bad release burn the budget and stop the next release. Press Next.

Where you meet it

      
Freshness of chain data measured as blocks behind head, not as time, because block time varies. Google SRE (site reliability engineering) practice made error budgets the rule that settles release speed between product and operations. Burn-rate alerts page when the budget will be gone in hours.

    

    

      
The practical choices

      
Measure the SLI where the user is, not inside one server. Pick the SLO from what users need: a free analytics tier can live at 99.9 percent, a payment rail cannot. Keep the SLA, the contract, looser than the SLO. Never promise more nines than your weakest hard dependency.
