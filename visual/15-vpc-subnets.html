Where you meet it: every cloud deployment

      
Load balancers and the bastion in public subnets, servers and databases in private ones, one subnet pair per availability zone so the network survives a zone loss.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: on bare metal

      
The same idea without the names: a private network for the cluster, one guarded entry point, firewalls that name their callers, and a check for open ports on the hosts.
