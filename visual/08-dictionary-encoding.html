Where you meet it

      
StarRocks low-cardinality optimisation: a global dictionary for string columns with few values. Parquet dictionary pages. ClickHouse LowCardinality(String).

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Keep columns like dex_name and status as strings and let the engine encode them. Expect no gain on hashes, addresses or ids, where every value is new.
