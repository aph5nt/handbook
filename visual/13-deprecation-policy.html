Where you meet it: public and internal APIs

      
Public APIs with paying clients often give six to twelve months. Internal APIs need weeks, because every caller shows up in the logs.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: agent tools

      
Keep the old tool name as an alias whose description says "deprecated, use X", log who still calls it, then remove it on the date.
