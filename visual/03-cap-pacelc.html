Where you meet it

      
A graph database cluster with one leader stops writes when it loses its peers: CP. A DNS (Domain Name System) cache keeps answering old records: AP. DynamoDB is PA/EL, a single-leader Postgres is PC/EC.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Decide per feature what a replica does when it cannot reach its peers: refuse, or answer with what it has. Then decide the normal-day trade: wait for the copies before replying, or reply at once and copy later.
