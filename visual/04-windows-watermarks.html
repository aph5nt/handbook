Windows and watermarks

A stream job counts swaps per pool per minute. Swaps arrive a little out of order, because nodes and networks lag. When is the 12:00 minute finished? Press Next to see windows group events by their own time, a watermark decide when to close a window, and a late swap arrive after the close.

Where you meet it

      
Kafka Streams, Apache Flink, Spark Structured Streaming and Apache Beam (Google Dataflow). All group by event time into windows and use a watermark or a grace period to decide when a window is final.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Group by event time, never by arrival time. Set the allowed lateness from measured delay, not a guess. Send late events to a side output and count them, so you know when the setting is wrong. Chapter 16 goes deeper.
