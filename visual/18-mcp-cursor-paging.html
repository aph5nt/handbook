Where you meet it

      
tools/list, resources/list, resources/templates/list and prompts/list all page this way. A gateway with many servers behind it. A resource list over a large store.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
As a server, make the cursor a keyset position, not an offset, so pages stay stable while the list changes. As a host, loop until no nextCursor.
