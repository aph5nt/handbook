Restart backoff and the poisoned message

Device actor 42 reads its configuration row in PreStart. A bad migration left that row corrupt. Every start throws. Press Next to see a plain restart turn that into a storm, then a backoff supervisor that waits, gives up and alerts. The last tab shows a poisoned message from outside, which goes to a dead-letter queue.

Where you meet it

      
Akka BackoffSupervisor around actors that touch databases or devices. Kubernetes CrashLoopBackOff is the same idea for pods. Kafka consumers send a poison record to a dead-letter topic so the partition can move on.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Exponential backoff from about a second to a ceiling such as 30 seconds, with jitter. A restart limit per time window, then stop and alert on the restart rate. Bad input from outside is parked in a dead-letter queue for a person, never retried for ever.
