Logical clocks

Two servers log 'user posted' and 'user banned' 3 ms apart. Their wall clocks disagree by 10 ms. Press Next to watch the log lie, then watch a counter put events back in the order they caused each other.

Where you meet it

      
On a blockchain the block height is the logical clock: two indexers on two machines agree on order without comparing wall time. Dynamo-style stores keep a vector clock per key to spot conflicting writes. CockroachDB orders transactions with a hybrid logical clock (HLC).

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Never order by wall clock across machines. Use a Lamport clock when you only need an order that respects cause. Use a vector clock when you must know that two writes were concurrent. Use HLC when humans must also read the stamp.
