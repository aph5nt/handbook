Where you meet orchestration

      
Money, compliance, anything support must explain. Temporal, Camunda or AWS Step Functions keep the orchestrator's state durable, so it resumes after a crash. The saga in chapter 3 is run by one of these two styles.

    

    

      
Where you meet choreography

      
Loose notifications, many teams, high volume: a block is indexed and whoever cares reacts. Keep a diagram of who produces and who consumes each event, because the code will not show the flow.
