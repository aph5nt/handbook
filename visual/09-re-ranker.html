Where you meet it

      
Cohere Rerank, open cross-encoder models such as the bge rerankers, and LLM-based re-ranking. The second stage of almost every production RAG pipeline. Search engines have used two-stage ranking for decades.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Retrieve wide and cheap, then re-rank narrow and expensive. Take about 50 candidates from hybrid search, re-rank only those, keep about 5. Measure the first stage by recall (is the right chunk in the 50?) and the second by precision (is it in the 5?).
