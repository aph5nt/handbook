Where you meet it

      
Every chat model you call went through these stages. A model card names them and their known limits. A base model, served raw, continues text instead of answering. A chat model agrees with a wrong claim in the prompt more often than you expect.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Do not trust tone as truth. Evals check facts in code. Prompts and skills make "I do not know" and "not found" good answers. When a model agrees too easily, give it the data and ask it to check the claim, not to confirm it.
