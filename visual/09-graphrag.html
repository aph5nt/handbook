Where you meet it

      
A graph sync copies chain facts from StarRocks (a columnar warehouse) into DozerDB (a Neo4j-compatible graph database). An agent walks it through graph_query, anchored on one address. Questions that are walks, "who is connected to whom, through what", belong here.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Build the graph from records, not from a model reading text. Anchor every walk, cap the hops and the time. Combine with text chunks when the answer also needs free-text detail. If you must extract a graph from text, give the extraction its own eval before anyone walks it.
