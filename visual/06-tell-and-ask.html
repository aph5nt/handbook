Where you meet tell

      
Everywhere inside an actor system. Akka Tell, Erlang cast, an Orleans one-way call. In the IoT backend every reading was a tell. The sender moves on at once and the receiver works at its own pace.

    

    

      
Where you meet ask

      
At the edge, where a caller outside the actors needs an answer: an HTTP handler, a gRPC endpoint. Akka Ask, Erlang call. Set the ask timeout shorter than the client's timeout. Inside a handler, tell with a reply-to and pipe futures back as messages.
