Where you meet it

      
Every framework in this chapter is this model with tools around it: Erlang processes, Akka and Akka.NET actors, Orleans grains, Akka Typed behaviours. Go's goroutines and channels are a cousin, CSP (Communicating Sequential Processes): there the channels have names, here the actors do.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Test each design against the three moves. A static cache, a singleton service shared by actors, or a mutable object inside a message is outside the model, and brings the races back. Put that state in an actor of its own.
