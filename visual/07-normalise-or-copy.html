Where you meet it

      
An app database (OLTP, Online Transaction Processing) sits in third normal form, so one update touches one row. A warehouse (OLAP, Online Analytical Processing) such as StarRocks repeats the block timestamp and the day on every transfer row, so a day filter needs no join.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Normalise where you write. Copy where you read, but make the copy a derived table that a pipeline rebuilds from the raw facts. Never patch a copy in place: the next copy you forgot will still hold the old value.
