Where you meet it

      
Heat maps, delivery zones, "orders near here". H3 libraries exist for most languages and several data warehouses ship H3 functions. Turning two location data sets into cell ids lets you join on an id instead of on distance.

    

    

      
The choice you make

      
Pick the cell size by the question: a city block for surge, a district for a weekly report. Keep the fine counts when exact totals matter, because children do not cover their parent exactly.
