Where you meet it: a repository

      
ADRs live next to the code, numbered: docs/adr/0012-graph-is-a-projection.md. Every schema change ships as a numbered migration gated by a runner. A gate fails a unit-test tier over three minutes, or any file over 1,500 lines.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: architecture tests

      
A lint fails when domain imports infrastructure. ArchUnit (Java) and import linters (Go, Python) do this. Evolutionary architecture: everything may change, but the fitness functions guard what matters.
