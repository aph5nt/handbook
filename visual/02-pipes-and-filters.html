Pipes and filters

Inside the indexer, each block goes through five stages. Each stage is a goroutine with a channel in and a channel out. When pricing slows down, the channels fill and fetching waits. Press Next.

Where you meet it: a Go indexer

      
Each stage is a goroutine and each pipe is a channel made with a capacity: make(chan Block, 3). A slow stage blocks the send before it. Scale one stage by starting more goroutines that read the same channel.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: Unix and streams

      
Unix shell pipes are the original pipes and filters. Kafka Streams and Flink are the same idea across machines. Inside one service it is a pipeline. Between services it is events: a pipeline is a chain, events are a bus.
