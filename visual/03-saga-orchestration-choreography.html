Saga orchestration versus choreography

The same phone order runs two ways. An orchestrator calls payment, stock and shipping in turn. Or there is no conductor and each service reacts to the last event. Press Next, and watch both handle a shipping failure.

Where you meet orchestration

      
Temporal, AWS Step Functions and Camunda. The plan is code or a diagram you can read. A subscription top-up with charge, grant and receipt fits here, because the refund path must be obvious.

    

    

      
Where you meet choreography

      
Services on Kafka reacting to each other's events. Good for loosely coupled teams and short flows of two or three steps. Each service still uses an outbox so its event is never lost.

Interview line"Orchestration puts the plan in one place I can read and debug. Choreography removes the centre but hides the flow, so I choreograph short flows and orchestrate long ones."
