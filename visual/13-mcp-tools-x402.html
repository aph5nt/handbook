Where you meet it: a local tool proxy

      
A thin MCP door over a normal API, as in Claude Desktop or Claude Code: typed inputs, read-only marks on read-only tools, clear error types such as anchor-missing, and every rule inside the tool's description. Chapter 18 covers MCP itself.

    

    

      
Where you meet it: paying per call

      
x402 between machines: a clear price per unit in the 402 answer, a payment on chain, a proof header, and a receipt the client can check. Chapter 14 covers the safety rings around agents.
