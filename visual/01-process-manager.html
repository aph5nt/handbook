Where you meet it

      
Subscription billing with retries and a grace period. An order that waits for payment, stock and shipping (the saga in chapter 3 is a process manager across services). Temporal or AWS Step Functions run process managers as durable workflows. A Kubernetes controller is a process manager: watch events, compare state, send the next command.

    

    

      
The practical choice

      
Use a policy when one event leads to one command and nothing must be remembered. Use a process manager when the process waits, branches or times out. Store its state like an aggregate, correlate by a business id, and give every path an ending, including the timeout.
